## public/models/wave/surfer.glb
Original work generated by `scripts/blender/build_surfer.py` (Blender 5.2, procedural: lofted anatomical figure with head 1/7.5 of height, fingered hands, hair, wetsuit seams, plus 6'0" board and leash). No third-party assets are included, so no external licence applies; same licence as this repository.

Third-party search (2026-10-29): no CC0/CC-BY rigged human of realistic quality could be fetched by direct download (Khronos CesiumMan is CC-BY 4.0 but is a 3k-triangle mannequin; Poly Pizza and Quaternius figures are stylised and sit behind interactive downloads; Mixamo/Ready Player Me forbid redistribution). The procedural figure was therefore improved instead.
