## Studies (agents, report only; no app change this round)
### 3D. A fairer rating measurement
The HI/East audit found that the observed-fed rating used the buoy's total wave height as the swell, so it mostly measured how it was built. California's audit has the same caveat.

**Redo both audits (California and HI/East) with the swell split from the wind-sea.** NDBC publishes swell and wind-wave height, period and direction (realtime2 `.spec`, and the historical swden/stdmet derived files). Where a buoy doesn't publish the split, the spot is left out and reported.

**Output:**
- `docs/forecast/RATING-AUDIT-SPLIT.md` and its JSON: how often each region gets "fair or better while poor" on split inputs, against the old method;
- a vault note.

**Proposals only:** any change to the rating engine is a separate spec.

### 3E. Australia groundwork
