## Problems carried from R12 (FINDINGS §14)
1. **Positioning:** at 5–6 ft, our rider trims standing in the lip's root, so he's crammed against the wave (the default view, `default/r12/f07`). Real surfers trim on the open face ahead of the curl and go under the lip only to get barrelled.
2. **Clipping:** bodyclip is still above 0 in 6 of 18 cells: the 6 ft hit's head and recovery hand, and single-sample hands and knees. The board clips 1–4 times at the 6 ft hit.
3. **Over-rotation:** after the hit, shoulders reach 66° against about 45° in real frames.
4. **Fluidity:**
   - the hands peak at 2.6–2.8 m/s out of the cutback;
   - transitions are config fades, not authored;
   - anticipation is limited to the existing 0.3 s lead;
   - the user: "most issues are on the backside … the arms and movements are still too jerky and abrupt … needs to be more fluid".

## Target, per item
1. **Positioning:**
   - in trim, pump and turns, the line sits on the open face ahead of the curl, at the face fraction the real clips show (Surf Ranch and the bottom-turn clip: about the middle third of the face, about one to two board lengths ahead of the breaking section);
   - only the barrel move enters the cavity;
   - the standing posture fits without any roof cap;
   - check the default view (5 ft Intermediate, seed 11) and 6 ft Expert.
2. **Per-move angles:** for each move (trim, bottom turn, top turn or snap, cutback, barrel, pop-up; front and backside), our median knee, hip, elbow and shoulder angles, torso lean, wrist height, hip height and stance width fall inside the real clips' 10–90% range. Put a table in FINDINGS §15: ours against the skeletons, per move.
3. **Timing and anticipation:** the phase timing follows the GIFs. Head and shoulders lead the turn by the real lead time; arms move together with the torso rotation, never on their own; the recovery settles at the real rate. Re-author the transitions (keys or curves) where the config fades can't do it.
4. **Fluidity:**
   - per move, the p95 angular velocity and acceleration of head, shoulders, elbows, wrists and spine come down further from R12, especially backside;
   - no peak above the real clips' estimated rates (a rough estimate from the skeleton time series, frame rate permitting);
   - the jerk gate stays 0.
5. **Clipping:** bodyclip 0 and board clip 0 in all 18 cells. Item 1 should remove most of it at the root.
6. **Over-rotation:** after the hit, shoulder yaw within the real range.
