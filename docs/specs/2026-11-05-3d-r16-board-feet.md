## Reference board (until the user's own board)
- A typical high-performance shortboard, **5'10" × 19¼" × 2⅜"** (about 28 L), squash tail, thruster. Take the outline, rocker, foil and rail **numbers** from public shaper spec sheets or dimension charts (cite 2–3 in FINDINGS §18).
- Reference photos (deck, bottom, side rocker, rail close-up) are for looking at only. They stay in the scratchpad and are never committed. No brand logos or graphics on our board.
- Every field carries `source: 'default'`, so the user's photographed board (T-136) can replace it field by field later.

## Items, in order
1. **One `buildBoard(params)`** (TypeScript) used by both the wave scene and the Quiver. The board baked into the surfer GLB goes away; the viewer builds the board and parents it to the `board` bone. The params:
   - length, width, thickness;
   - the outline curve (wide point, nose and tail widths);
   - the rocker (nose and tail lift) and the foil;
   - the rail profile;
   - the tail (squash, round, swallow, pin);
   - the fins (thruster, quad, twin);
   - a `source` per field.
   Saved Quiver boards render through the same function with defaults. Old caches get a fallback.
2. **The shape** (3–6k tris):
   - a rounded nose tip and a proper tail cut;
   - a visible stringer;
   - tucked-edge rails (soft forward, harder toward the tail);
   - a single-to-double concave bottom;
   - foiled fins (a flat inside face, with fin boxes), facing outward as in T-139.
3. **Materials** (procedural, no image downloads):
   - a glossy resin (low roughness, clearcoat);
   - a white deck with a slightly translucent stringer;
   - **wax** only in the foot zone: a bumpy normal and a matte roughness;
   - a tail pad on the back foot (EVA grooves, kick).
   Must read clearly at the default camera on 1440 and 390.
4. **Feet planted on the deck:**
   - each sole follows the deck's dome and the rocker under it (IK reads the deck height from `buildBoard`), within 6 mm and never through the deck, in every key and through the rail rolls;
   - the back foot sits on the tail pad, square across the stringer; the front foot is angled ~15–25°;
   - a soft contact-shadow blob under each foot on the deck;
   - if the MPFB foot is a flat slab, shape a toe/arch read (no finger work: that's phase 3).
5. **Checks:**
   - foot-contact numbers per key, before and after;
   - the 18-cell glitch matrix still bodyclip 0 / gates 0 (R15 levels at worst);
   - the barrel times are kept;
   - 60 fps;
   - the Quiver card and its preview still render (journeys).
