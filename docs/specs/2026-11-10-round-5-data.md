## New
1. **NWPS phase 2: nearshore heights for the East Coast, Gulf and Pacific Northwest.** NOMADS server-side subset (≈80 KB), then the 5 m and 20 m contour text files. Covers 148 of 265 non-Hawaii spots without a MOP point. Before use: re-pick cells per run (v1.5 grids), and ask NOAA whether the 20 m-line Hs is measured at 20 m. Labelled "NWS NWPS nearshore model, unvalidated" until scored at a buoy.
2. **More observed wind.**
   - US East and Gulf shore wind from NDBC C-MAN and NOS met stations, behind an enclosed-water mask (the reason East ASOS was held back).
   - World spots: nearest METAR airports (IEM), listed as context only.
3. **More buoys in the archive and the How-right check:** the Gulf and Puerto Rico buoys added by batch 3 (CariCOOS/NDBC). The archive gets a local backfill (NDBC only, no Open-Meteo).
4. **Observed tides for world spots:** IOC sea-level monitoring and UHSLC gauge observations within 50 km, shown as "observed water level at <gauge>", never as a prediction. Licences checked per network.
5. **Ireland into the app** (the readers already exist):
   - spots from ireland.json with the app rule (guide facing, a Marine Institute tide station, a buoy within range);
   - "not available in this region" for alerts;
   - tide heights wait until the Marine Institute confirms the datum.
6. **Surf-cam coverage beyond the Bay Area:** the same "usable now" search (US public domain, owner-enabled YouTube embeds, explicit embed terms) for Southern California, Hawaii and the East Coast. No Surfline in any form.
7. **Catalogue: the remaining recoverable drops.** The 906 ambiguous-coastline drops get a second pass using the guide's own wind or swell directions as a constraint on a longer coastline window (500 m to 1.5 km), and are recovered only if the guide's published directions agree within 30°. Otherwise they stay dropped.
