# Map: Windy colours, a heatmap that works on phone and while zooming, less AI chrome
Status: **APPROVED** (user, 2026-11-04: "fix that … on phone it doesnt seem like the heatmap even works … the colors are so weird use the same colors as your reference windy … when I zoom out the heatmap goes gray and then restores … the menus look pretty AI … make a spec for this and get to working").

## Context (lead, /explore on :5180, 1440 and 390; shots in the scratchpad `map/`)
- **The heat field** is a canvas (`xfield-field` + `xfield-flow`, `src/map/field.ts`, `SpatialLayer.ts`, colours `src/map/colors.ts`) over a Leaflet Esri basemap. It's masked to the open sea (T-092), with a time scrubber and a legend.
- **Colours:** T-109 changed the ramp to one pale-to-navy hue at 0.5 opacity (site critique, RULES "no rainbow"). The user overrides this: use Windy's wave colours (`docs/design/TEARDOWN-2026-10-31.md` §Windy; reference shots `~/Development/swello-design-references/…/windy/1440-0x-*.png`, `390-0x-*.png`).
- **Zoom:** mid zoom-out the field disappears and the grey basemap tile grid shows (`zooming-1440.png`); the field returns only after the zoom ends. It's redrawn on `moveend`/`zoomend` and isn't transformed with the zoom animation.
- **Phone (390):**
  - the field draws but reads as one flat blue (no visible gradient);
  - the bottom sheet covers about 45% of the screen;
  - the legend is squeezed into a pill;
  - not yet checked on a real phone (DPR 3, touch, iOS Safari canvas size limits).
- **Chrome:** the floating "Spots 45" dropdown, the layer popover, rounded white pill labels on every spot ("Unrated"), and the stacked floating buttons read as generic or AI.

## Problems → target
1. **Colours = Windy's wave-height ramp.**
   - Sample the stops from the Windy reference shots and legend (calm deep blue → blue → cyan → green → yellow → orange → red → magenta for big seas).
   - Same stops in the legend. Opacity tuned so the coastline and basemap still read (about 0.65–0.75), with land fully unpainted.
   - The legend keeps "Offshore wave height · not surf size" (data honesty) and the unit toggle.
   - Record the user's override of RULES "no rainbow" in `docs/design/RULES.md` (maps use the Windy ramp by user decision, 2026-11-04).
2. **Zoom and pan never show grey.**
   - During zoom and pan, the existing field canvas is CSS-transformed with the map animation (Leaflet `zoomanim` / `_animateZoom` pattern); the redraw at the end swaps in without a blank frame.
   - Pan keeps the canvas positioned. Particles pause during the animation and resume.
   - No grey basemap gap at any zoom from 5 to 12.
3. **The phone heatmap works and reads.**
   - Verify on iPhone emulation (390×844 DPR 3, touch) and Android (412×915 DPR 2.6).
   - Cap the canvas size to device limits (draw at DPR ≤ 2, scale up).
   - Make sure the gradient is visible at the default zoom: the colour domain fits the visible values, the legend shows the domain, and there's no single flat colour.
   - The bottom sheet starts as a peek (≤ 25% of the height) and expands on tap or drag; the map gets the rest.
4. **Less AI chrome (Windy-like, function first).**
   - **Layers:** a compact vertical layer list on the right edge (Waves · Swell · Wind · Period), plain icons and text labels, an active state as a filled row. No floating card with a heavy shadow.
   - **Spots:** labels only for the selected spot and the top-rated few at the current zoom. Others are small rating-coloured dots. Hover/tap shows name + surf height + rating. No "Unrated" pill everywhere; the night/dark state is a single quiet mark.
   - **Spot filter:** the search plus the region chips live in one compact top bar on the map (Windy-style), not a floating "Spots 45" dropdown.
   - **Bottom:** the timeline scrubber and the legend sit in one bottom strip, matching Windy's layout at 1440 and 390.
   - Tap targets stay ≥ 44 px (T-109). Dark mode works.

## Constraints
- Data honesty: the legend and labels say offshore wave height, not surf size; "Not covered" stays on enclosed waters; no invented values.
- Performance: 60 fps panning and zooming at 1440 on desktop; ≥ 30 fps on the phone emulation with 4× CPU throttle; no new map library; Leaflet stays.
- Open-Meteo quota: QA runs stay light (a few page loads), no click storms (yesterday's 429).

## Owners and level
- **Engineering** (Level 2, branch `t115-map-field`): items 1, 2, 3, i.e. the field rendering, colours, zoom transform, DPR cap, colour domain and RULES note.
- **Product** (Level 2, branch `t116-map-chrome`): item 4 plus the phone bottom-sheet peek. It starts in parallel; files split: engineering `src/map/*` + `SpatialLayer`, product `src/features/explore/*` + `explore.css`.
- **Lead:** gate, side-by-side with the Windy shots, and phone emulation checks.

## Acceptance
- Desktop and phone shots side by side with the Windy reference shots, at the same area and zoom. The colour stops match the sampled ones (unit test on colors.ts).
- A zoom-out from 10 to 6 captured mid-animation: no grey, the field visible (toolkit check `t115-zoom.mjs`: sample canvas pixels over sea mid-zoom ≠ basemap grey).

# §5 Windy rebuild (T-117): the map closely modelled on Windy's
Status: **APPROVED** (user, 2026-11-04: "I want it to be closely modeled like windy's map, same colors as those are pretty universal, and if the other changes are working then the windy rebuild can take place, make spec and apply"). **Gate:** starts when T-115 round 2 (opaque field on a grey basemap, no grey while zooming) and T-116 (chrome) are merged and verified at 1440 and 390.

## References
- Live Windy, captured by the lead (same area, 2026-11-03 7 PM): scratchpad `map/windy-live/` (`waves-1568-big-swell.jpg`, `point-popup-particles.jpg`, `legend.png`).
- `~/Development/swello-design-references/18-teardown-2026-10-31/windy/` (1440 and 390, layer list, timeline, point forecast, model picker).
- `docs/design/TEARDOWN-2026-10-31.md` §Windy.

## Target (match Windy's map closely; keep only what data honesty requires)
1. **Colour field:**
   - Windy's wave ramp (ft 1.6 teal · 3.3 light blue · 5 blue · 6.6 indigo · 20 magenta-red · 30 salmon), opaque over the sea, with smooth interpolation (no banding);
   - the same ramps for Swell (height), Wind (Windy's wind ramp) and Period (Windy's period ramp), each with its own legend.
2. **Particles:** short white dashes drifting with the wave or swell direction (waves layer) and streamlines for wind, at Windy's density and speed. "Particles animation" toggle; reduced motion = still.
3. **Basemap:** flat mid-grey land, white place labels, thin dark coastline, no colour on land. Dark theme: the same, darker.
4. **Right-edge layer list:** round thumbnail icons with labels (Waves · Swell · Wind · Period · More), the active one highlighted, as on Windy. Phone: a menu button opens the same list as a sheet.
5. **Bottom timeline:**
   - a full-width day strip (Tue 3, Wed 4, … across the forecast range);
   - a play button on the left;
   - an hour bubble above the playhead ("7 PM");
   - the legend chip at bottom right with the unit inside it (tap cycles ft/m);
   - the model chip beside it naming our real source ("CDIP MOP · Open-Meteo"; honest, never "ECMWF" unless it is).
6. **Point tap:**
   - a small dark pin label at the point, like Windy's: "12 ft W · 11 s" for the layer's value, direction and period, with an × and an expand chevron;
   - expand opens the bottom forecast panel: an hourly table (time, wind + gust in speed-coloured cells with arrows, waves, swell 1/2, period, tide) like Windy's Waves & Tides tab, with an "Open full forecast" link to our spot page.
   - Spots keep their rating dots and labels (our addition).
7. **Top left:** the search box with a locate button, as on Windy. Our app header stays.
8. **Phone (390):** Windy's phone layout: search on top, the map, floating round buttons, a compact timeline with the legend under it, and the point forecast as a bottom sheet with a horizontally scrolling table.

## Keep (data honesty and our rules)
- The legend and point label say what the value is: **"Offshore wave height · not surf size"** (the field is offshore Hs, not breaking surf). The surf estimate stays on the spot pin and card.
- "Not covered" on enclosed bays, where we have no data. Windy paints them; we don't invent values.
- 44 px tap targets, keyboard and aria, reduced motion.
- No Windy branding, logo, fonts or copied assets: we match the layout, interaction and universal colour scales, drawn by us.

## Owners and level
- **Level 3.** Product owns the chrome, timeline, point panel and phone layout (`src/features/explore/*`). Engineering owns the field, particles, ramps, basemap and point values (`src/map/*`). One branch each, merged in order: engineering, then product.
- Fable isn't needed. Design-critic pass at the end.

## Acceptance
- Side-by-side PNGs: ours vs Windy live at the same area, zoom and time, at 1440 and 390, light (and dark for ours). The lead signs off that it reads as "Windy's map".
- The colour stops match the sampled legend (unit test). Opaque sea, grey land.
- Particles at 60 fps desktop, ≥ 30 fps phone emulation (4× CPU).
- No grey frames while zooming (t115-zoom).
- Point tap → label → panel → the spot page works by mouse, touch and keyboard.
- Checks: tsc, tests and build; t043-map (updated, with backups); journeys once the Open-Meteo quota allows.
