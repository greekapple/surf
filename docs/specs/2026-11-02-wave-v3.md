# Wave scale 3D, v3: a wave that actually breaks, different per size, lit by the hour

Status: APPROVED (user, 2026-11-02, "proceed" on the lead's plan; original user brief with problems 1–7 and per-bucket references is quoted in the lead's plan in the session). Branch `wave-v3`. Caption "3D illustration · approximate scale, not current conditions" stays.

## Current pipeline (facts)
- `tools/blender/wave_scene.py` builds the scene from the licensed TurboSquid 1773957 cross-sections (`ond2.fbx`/`ond3.fbx` rows: HUMP=ond3[125], MID=ond2[70], BARREL=ond2[15]; 171-point grid rows; git-ignored assets in `tools/blender/assets/turbosquid-1773957/`). `soften_trough` removes the ditch, `profile(c, amp)` blends stages and normalises to face height, and `along(x)` sets curl/amp/foam along the crest. `--glb` exports a static mesh + JSON (surfer matrix) to `public/media/scale3d-live/` (git-ignored, licensed-derived). Surfer: `tools/blender/assets/surfer-v2.glb` (MPFB CC0, `build_surfer.py`).
- `src/features/scale/WaveLive.tsx` (three.js) loads `<band>.glb` + `.json` + `surfer.glb` and runs a custom water shader on a static mesh, ~1,300 spray sprites, sea quads, a sky dome and OrbitControls. `ScaleStage.tsx` picks the bucket (hour estimate midpoint or size chip). Hour/sun and board selector don't drive 3D.

## Baseline problems (verified by lead, P0 shots in `~/Development/ai-system-setup/playwright-toolkit/swello/artifacts/wave-v3/before/`)
1. Uniform extruded cylinder; neon green along the whole crest. 2. Cotton-like whitewater. 3. The lip never pitches; no curl, pocket or feathering. 4. Plastic face: no texture, draining lines or chop. 5. Glitter sparkles on the face. 6. White wall/fin: background set waves are clipped at the export crop (`R = 18H+12`), leaving a vertical cliff. 7. Buckets are the same wave at a different scale. Also: re-clicking a selected size chip deselects it (ScaleStage `setBand(band === i ? null : i)`). Surfer bbox is constant (1.69 m) across buckets; fps 30 in headless.

## Plan
- **P1 profile atlas (Blender, `wave_scene.py --atlas`).** Per bucket, a break-cycle table: ~12 stages × 96 points (arc-length resampled), each point (y, z, thin, foam) in metres, written as a small binary or PNG (float16) + JSON meta, ~100–300 KB/bucket.
  - Asymmetric (sharp crest, broad trough).
  - Family per bucket, with real-world models:
    - 1–2 / 2–3 ft: spilling beach-break crumbler (foam rolls down a gentle slope, little open face).
    - 3–4 / 4–6 ft: Lower Trestles peeler (peak, tapering shoulder, lip feathers then pitches).
    - 6–8 / 8–10 ft: heavier beach break at size (thick throwing lip, pocket, big foam ball, back-spray).
    - 10–15 ft: Sunset/Waimea (very thick lip, massive whitewater, mist).
    - 15+ ft: Mavericks/Jaws scale.
  - Break point tied to H ≈ 0.78 × depth on a per-bucket shoaling slope, which sets where the peak breaks and the peel speed.
  - Cross-sections stay derived from the licensed rows (the atlas is licensed-derived: git-ignored, never committed).
- **P2 GPU-driven live wave.** WaveLive uses a plain grid; the vertex shader samples the atlas by (u, stage(x, t)):
  - per-section stage offsets so it peels down the line on a loop (feather → pitch → lip lands → foam ball grows and advances);
  - a peak/shoulder height envelope along the crest. Never a uniform extrusion.
  - Replaces the static GLB wave.
- **P3 look.**
  - Colour by thickness: only the thin, backlit lip and upper crest go turquoise; the thick base is deep blue-green to near-black.
  - Fresnel sky + sun glint; capillary chop and wind texture; draining lines before the break.
  - Background swell from a Blender Ocean-modifier TMA-spectrum bake (small tiling height/normal/foam textures).
- **P4 foam & spray.**
  - Foam: a lumpy aerated foam ball with grey self-shadowing; lacy trails that drift and thin.
  - Spray only off the lip, plus offshore back-spray and mist over the whitewater; no face glitter.
- **P5 hour + board.**
  - Sun/sky from the solar position at the spot and hour (6 PM in Nov = twilight, rendered honestly).
  - The selected Quiver board at true length/width/thickness replaces the surfer's board.
  - The surfer is fixed at 1.75 m in every bucket.
- **P6 stills** regenerated from the same atlas for posters. Propose dropping the 24-frame Spin360 stills (24 MB) if live covers it.
- **P7 verification.** `wave-v3-shots.mjs` (toolkit) gives same-angle 6 PM shots per bucket side by side, a full camera sweep including the former white-wall angle, constant surfer size, and fps + asset size before/after.
- **Fixes:** the white wall (crop/sets), the chip toggle.
