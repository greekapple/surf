# Wind now: measured shore wind next to the forecast (2026-11-08)

User: "get the agent worktrees and product and engineering involved … criticise your own work … automate this for around 40 minutes, make a spec and after you're finished make a report." This follows the wind-point study (`docs/forecast/WIND-POINT.md`):
- The forecast's wind cell could not be settled, because no station stands at the beaches.
- The audits now read shore stations within 7 km instead of offshore buoys.

## Problem
A surfer can't see whether the wind forecast is right **now**. The app shows only modelled wind. The offshore buoys it reads (46026, 46012) are 18–24 NM out and are shown for waves only. Surfline shows a measured wind where it has one. We have public shore stations next to several spots and use none of them in the app.

## Change
1. **Engineering (worktree, Opus):** a shore-wind registry and a live feed.
   - **`src/ocean/shoreWind.ts`:**
     - The station for each spot, by rule: the nearest shore station within **4 km** of the spot's coordinate, of one of two kinds:
       - a fixed NDBC/NOS met station outside enclosed water (`src/map/enclosed.ts`);
       - an airport ASOS.
     - Generated by a script (`docs/forecast/data/build-shore-wind.ts`, reusing `docs/forecast/data/shore-wind.ts`) into a committed `src/ocean/shoreWind.json`: id, name, kind, lat, lon, distance, and the source URL.
     - The display rule (4 km) is stricter than the audit rule (7 km): an airfield 6 km inland is not shown as the wind at the beach.
   - **Live readings** go through the existing provider service (`services/forecast-service.mjs` `ingestionTargets`; the Cloudflare worker uses the same file):
     - NDBC: `realtime2/<ID>.txt` (WDIR, WSPD, GST).
     - ASOS: the NWS API `api.weather.gov/stations/K<ID>/observations/latest`, with a generic `User-Agent` and no personal contact.
     - TTL 10–15 min.
   - **Normaliser:** returns `ShoreWindNow | null`:
     ```ts
     interface ShoreWindNow {stationId: string; stationName: string; kind: 'ndbc' | 'asos'; distanceKm: number;
       observedAt: string; mph: number | null; gustMph: number | null; dirDeg: number | null; source: SourceInfo}
     ```
     - A reading older than 2 h returns null: stale is shown as missing, never as now.
     - Variable or missing direction → `dirDeg: null`.
   - **Tests:** a parser for each feed (fixtures), staleness, the selection rule, and that spots without a station get none.
   - **Owned files:**
     - new: `src/ocean/shoreWind.ts`, `src/ocean/shoreWind.json`, `docs/forecast/data/build-shore-wind.ts`, `src/tests/shore-wind-now.test.ts`, fixtures;
     - edited: `services/forecast-service.mjs` (ingestion targets plus one route only), and the hook-up in `src/ocean/pipeline.ts` or a new `src/services/shoreWindNow.ts`.
2. **Product (worktree, Opus):** a "Wind now" line on Forecast.
   - **Where:** in the spot's conditions area next to the forecast WIND.
     - It reads, for example, "Measured 14 mph W, gust 19 · Half Moon Bay airport, 2.1 km · 10:55 AM".
     - It sits beside the forecast for the same hour: "forecast 11 mph WNW".
   - **Kind note:** one muted note says what the station is. For an airport: "airfield wind, can read lighter than at the water". For a pier or headland: "pier station".
   - **No station:** nothing is shown. No placeholder, and no "unavailable" noise.
   - **Stale:** shown as "No reading in the last 2 h".
   - **Fixture:** build against a fixture of the `ShoreWindNow` interface above, so the product agent doesn't wait for engineering.
   - **Owned files:** new `src/features/forecast/WindNow.tsx` plus its CSS in `forecast.css`, and one mount line in `SpotPage.tsx` or `ForecastView.tsx`.
3. **Lead:**
   - Merges engineering first, then product, into `forecast-trust`.
   - Runs the gate, looks at 1440/390 light and dark at Mavericks (station), Crescent City (pier) and Ocean Beach (none).
   - Runs the data-honesty review.
   - Writes the report.

## Acceptance
- At least 4 spots get a station within 4 km, by rule; the list is in `shoreWind.json`. Ocean Beach, Linda Mar and Bolinas show none.
- At Mavericks and Crescent City, Forecast shows a measured wind with its station, its distance and the observation time in Pacific time, next to the forecast for that hour.
- The units match the forecast (mph, direction from).
- A stale reading or a missing feed shows no number. The console stays clean.
- No new layout shift at 390: the line fits the conditions area with no overflow; t034 density passes.
- tsc, tests, build, journeys 8/8, t043, t062, t104 and t034 pass. Data-honesty review: no P0, and P1s fixed.

## Level and limits
- Level 2: a new data source in the UI.
- Not deployed without the user's "deploy".
- Not a validation: a station reading is context, not the wind at the break. The copy must not imply the forecast is wrong or right from one reading.
- Self-critique checkpoints:
  - the lead reads both diffs line by line before merging;
  - the lead names at least one thing it would change in each handoff, and fixes or records it.
