# Shared contracts
Read before changing code. Whoever changes one of these updates this file in the same change and
tells the lead, who relays it to affected owners.

## Routing (`src/app/routes.ts`)
- `Area = 'home' | 'forecast' | 'explore' | 'quiver' | 'personal'`: exactly five primary destinations.
- Wave Scale is the Forecast section `wave-scale` (`/forecast/wave-scale`); `/scale…` is redirected by `parseRoute`.
- `navigate(area, section)` / `go` updates URL and state; `?spot=<slug>` carries the selected spot.

## Shared state (`src/app/store` → `useSwello()`)
`spot`, `setSpot`, `spotOptions`, `series` (hourly samples for the spot), `view.selectedTime` / `view.selectTime`,
`view.mapRecords`, `view.ocean` (bundle, loading, error, statuses), `data.state` (profile, boards, favorites,
sessions; persisted to localStorage key `surf-prototype-v1`), `data.favorite(id)`, `live`, `now`.

## Data honesty
- `heightFt` from live providers is **offshore significant wave height**; label it "offshore".
- Breaking-wave (face) values exist only where `forecast.availability.face` is true; otherwise show
  "unavailable" or "illustrative", never a number.
- Nearshore model (CDIP MOP; Ocean Beach `spot-2` SF023 reviewed, since T-073 the points assigned by the facing-match rule at 39 more spots, each labelled so, and since T-130 five points chosen by us from CDIP shore normals, **not owner-reviewed** (Mavericks SM306, Cowell's SC149, Shelter Cove HU029, Rincon B0001, Bolinas MA137: all 45 spots now have a point, 44 distinct, each with its provenance and, for Mavericks, Cowell's and Bolinas, a caveat shown beside the number and in the Nearshore panel); the nowcast is fetched for SF023 only, so "nowcast nearby" (`HeadlineSurf.nowcastNearby`: a CDIP nowcast, the nearshore model run on measured buoy waves at the point, never a measurement at the spot) can only appear for Ocean Beach) is labelled "Nearshore model Hs at 10 m depth (CDIP MOP SF023)", with its own valid time and location. Since T-077 (user decision "Surfer convention") that same Hs is also the headline surf-height estimate where the spot is on v2, and the panel says so; it is still a model value, not a measurement. It is shown only for the record within 30 min of the selected hour (nowcast window now ±24 h); otherwise say so, never extrapolate. Other spots: "No nearshore model for this spot". Never call it surf, face or breaking height.
- A `breaking-estimate` may be presented only with a range and a method: guard `canPresentBreakingEstimate` in `src/utils/waveHeight.ts` (the kinds are `WaveMeasurementKind`; no parallel type). The guard also requires a range at least 1 ft wide and a versioned method string (contains `v<number>`).
- Surf-height estimate (T-077): the headline height is the common surf-report convention, about the nearshore significant wave height (v2: the CDIP MOP Hs at the point; v1: the TOTAL significant height carried to 10 m by linear shoaling since T-081; the swell partition alone ran 14% below MOP Hs), labelled "Est. X–Y ft surf height · model estimate". The larger breaking height (`HeadlineSurf.breakingFt`) is shown only as a labelled detail ("breaking height about X"). Calibration pairs carry `basis: 'surf-height'`. Where a nearshore record exists for the hour and our rules refuse it (outside the window, short period, no break) or the spot has no breaking profile, the spot shows MOP's own nearshore height at its point (T-130 `nearshoreOnly`; replaces the T-077 offshore fallback): the same band, method line "MOP nearshore (CDIP), N m modelled point <id>, unvalidated" with the reason, the guide basis, the point's provenance and caveat, and "local shelter between the point and the break is not modelled"; under 0.5 ft (`FLAT_BELOW_FT`, our judgement) the headline is "Flat" (no number, no confidence word, no glyph; an all-flat day reads Flat). The offshore height stays in the detail. The rating is not fed by this number (it keeps judging the offshore swell) and says so in its explanation. The range's width comes from `src/services/uncertainty.ts`: the measured forecast error of the wave models against deep-water buoys by lead time (14-day, one-fortnight measurement, a floor, not a confidence interval; `docs/forecast/RANGE-T078.md`), widened to the bias-corrected disagreement between models, rounded outward to whole feet. It does not include wave-to-wave variation inside a set.
- **The range is the surf band (T-118, user decision 2026-11-04 "use surfline for reference ... a little differentiated ... beach oriented").** `HeadlineSurf.loFt`/`hiFt` are NOT an uncertainty interval: low = the typical wave (the convention surf height above, rounded down to whole feet), high = the sets (about 1.27 times it, the Rayleigh H1/10; nudged a little by beach: point or reef +0.04, plunging +0.05 checked slope / +0.025 mean slope, spilling -0.04; clamped 1.18 to 1.36; all of it OUR OWN JUDGEMENT, not fitted to local data), snapped to Surfline-style reporting widths (our approximation of their convention) (`src/services/surfBand.ts` WIDTH_TABLE: under 2 ft 1 ft, 2 to 6 ft 1 to 2, 6 to 10 ft 2 to 3, 10 to 15 ft 3, 15 ft and up 5). The surface must not call it a confidence interval or a guarantee. The forecast uncertainty is kept whole in `HeadlineSurf.interval` (loFt, hiFt, halfWidth, components, disagree) and shown as ONE confidence word beside the number (never beside a saved, stale or calibrated number) (`HeadlineSurf.confidence`, `headlineText().confidenceText`): high up to a half-width of 0.20, medium up to 0.30, low beyond, when the wave models disagree, or when the record has no breakdown; the reason and the full interval are in `confidenceDetail` (Why, tooltips). The thresholds are our own judgement and the word is NOT validated: in the 14-day backtest (VALIDATION.md 1c) the observed height fell inside the band about half the time (about nine in ten within one foot) whether the word was high or low, so tooltips and Why say so. Calibrated estimates (the person's own observations) keep their own measured range and show no word. The rating's uncertainty ceiling still reads the interval. One function feeds every surface (they all read `headlineSurf`); `surfBand(spotId, hour)` is the read API. Coverage of the band against the MOP nowcast and buoys is in docs/forecast/VALIDATION.md section 1c and is lower than the interval's by design.
- Validation evidence (T-089): `docs/forecast/VALIDATION.md` is a model-vs-model backtest plus a forward archive (`docs/forecast/data/validation-archive.jsonl`); it is NOT surfer-observed evidence and is not cited in user-facing text. Refresh with `docs/forecast/data/validate-surf-height.ts`.
- Breaking-wave estimate range (T-008; the legacy range stored on `OceanRecord.faceEstimate`; since T-081 Board Match, the estimate view and the rating's uncertainty ceiling read the convention height and its range via `src/services/surfRange.ts`, v1-based, which can differ from the v2 headline where MOP data exists): `OceanRecord.faceEstimate` (`{input}` or `{refusal}`), built by `src/ocean/breakingEstimate.ts` from primary swell and a reviewed per-spot profile in `src/ocean/localCalibration.ts`. It is an unvalidated range (label it an estimate, never measured, never alongside a single "face" number), is separate from the single-valued `face`, and is not an input to ratings. Ocean Beach (`spot-2`) has the reviewed profile. Since T-071 spots whose published spot guides (Surfnerd, Surf-Forecast.com) state a facing and swell range, get a profile built by `src/ocean/spotFactsRules.ts` from `src/ocean/spotFactsSourced.ts` (41 of 45 spots since T-073, provenance 'published guide', generic 8 s minimum period, unvalidated); a spot a source calls sheltered gets shelter 0 (the offshore-swell v1 refuses) and estimates only through its CDIP point inside the open sector a guide lists; a spot whose published working swell lies entirely outside ±60° of its facing (wrap: Mavericks, Rincon) refuses; one facing (Pismo) is derived from CDIP shore normals with provenance 'derived from coastline geometry'; every other spot refuses with a reason (`docs/forecast/ESTIMATE-COVERAGE-T073.md`). MOP points other than Ocean Beach's SF023 (39 spots since T-073) are assigned by a facing-match rule, not by review. A guide-derived estimate must say where its inputs came from (`guideBasis` in `src/services/headlineSurf.ts`). Method and validation limits: `docs/forecast/T-008-validation.md`.
- Every observed or modelled variable shows its own valid/observed time; one shared time only when all values share it. Retrieval time is never shown as model update/issue time. Retrieved records carry `confidence:'Unrated'`, `updatedAt:'Not supplied'`; `'Demo'` is only for the static demo dataset.
- Ratings are provisional and uncalibrated; say so where shown.
- No live camera imagery unless an authorised feed exists.

## Map field: what the colour is and is not (map-seamless Stage 2a)
- The field is NOAA WW3 (waves, swell, period) and GFS (wind) at their native 0.5° grid, served by `/api/ocean` (ERDDAP `.dods`, packed Int16, cached in memory 30 min per field+hour per server instance, so ERDDAP is fetched once per field per 30 min per instance, not per user; the response echoes the requested valid time, which for these 3-hourly datasets is a stored step). The California frame is a faster-loading subset of the SAME WW3/GFS 0.5° grid, blended into the global frame over 1.5° at its edge so no rectangle shows (`mapConfig.fill.nestFeatherDeg`).
- **Coastal fill:** cells the model leaves empty next to the coast (land mask, ice, the 0.5° coastline) are filled from their nearest ocean neighbours at most **2 cells (about 110 km) deep** (`mapConfig.fill.coastCells`), display only, never a reading (the pin label returns nothing in a filled cell); beyond that the field stays empty. It was 8 passes (up to 16°).
- **Enclosed water** (bays, harbours, estuaries) has no model value: it is transparent, with no hatch, no label and no colour. Nothing is blurred into it.
  The mask (`src/map/enclosed-waters.json`, 0.01° cells, read by `enclosed.ts`; the pin label and both map engines use it) is built from the repo's own Natural Earth coastline plus OpenStreetMap ocean water near the bays (tidal creeks, sloughs, salt ponds: `docs/forecast/data/enclosed-waters-osm.ts`; derived from © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, via OpenFreeMap tiles) with one rule: water far from the open Pacific is enclosed. Named mouths are explicit: everything west of the Golden Gate line (Point Bonita to Lands End) is open ocean. Tests: `src/tests/enclosed-waters.test.ts`.
- The map colour is open-ocean model height, not surf size at a spot; the legend names the layer actually painted.
