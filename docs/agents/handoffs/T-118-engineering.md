# T-118 handoff (engineering): the surf band and the confidence word

Branch `t118-surf-band` (forecast-trust merged). tsc clean, 499 tests (+7, `src/tests/t118-surf-band.test.ts`; one old interval test updated), build OK. Browser (:5185, 1440 and 390, one load each): Ocean Beach spot card "10–13 ft · model estimate · high confidence", tooltip with the reason and the full interval; no console errors. A data-honesty review ran (subagent `data-honesty-reviewer`); its P1s and the useful P2s are fixed (below).

## What changed
- **The range is the surf band** (`src/services/surfBand.ts`, pure): low = the typical wave (the convention surf height, rounded down to whole feet), high = the sets = typical x 1.27 (Rayleigh H1/10), nudged a little by beach (point/reef +0.04; plunging +0.05 on a checked slope, +0.025 on a mean slope; spilling -0.04; clamped 1.18 to 1.36), snapped to the width table (<2 ft: 1; 2 to 6: 1 to 2; 6 to 10: 2 to 3; 10 to 15: 3; 15+: 5). lo <= typical <= hi always. **All of this is our own judgement** (said in the method label, tooltip and CONTRACTS); the widths are Surfline-style, our approximation of the convention the user named.
- **One function feeds every surface**: `headlineSurf`'s `loFt/hiFt` ARE the band now, so Home, the cards, strip, week grid (midday hour), spot page and day cards, Compare, Find Spots and the map callout switch with no per-surface logic (a test checks spot hour, Home card, strip, day range, week cell and the Compare label agree). The API for the graph: `surfBand(spotId, hour, ctx)` / `surfBandOf(headline)` -> `{loFt, hiFt, centralFt, setsFt, confidence, interval, calibrated}` (announced earlier).
- **Uncertainty -> the confidence word** next to the number (spot card, Home, hero caption, Compare, Find Spots, callout, week-cell aria): high up to a half-width of 0.20 (about the first three days of the measured lead table, one source of error), medium up to 0.30, low beyond or when the wave models disagree or the record has no breakdown. The old interval stays whole in `HeadlineSurf.interval` and in the tooltip / Why ("the model forecast may land anywhere from 8–12 ft; the band above is the typical wave to the sets, not this interval"). The rating's uncertainty ceiling still reads the interval, unchanged.
- **Honesty fixes from the review**: no word beside a saved, stale or calibrated number (gated once, in `shownConfidence`); the visible word is "high confidence" (not a bare "high"); the tooltip says the word is our own judgement and NOT validated; a record with no band no longer claims one; typed `HeadlineText` (no `as any`).
- Docs: CONTRACTS.md has the T-118 bullet; `docs/forecast/VALIDATION.md` section 1c and the harness score the band (`npm run validate`, the forward archive now stores the band plus the interval; old entries score as the interval).

## Validation (T-089 harness re-run, 14-day backtest, `--no-archive`; v1 chain, so not the live v2 centre)
- Against the MOP nowcast: observed inside the band 50% at every lead; **inside the band ± 1 ft 88 to 89%**; 47% of observations fall BELOW the band (our central runs about 13% above MOP, obs ÷ central 0.87), 3 to 4% above; median band width 1.0 ft (most of those hours are small, under 2 to 3 ft). Against the buoy-derived value: 54 to 59% inside, 91 to 92% within ±1 ft, 38 to 43% ABOVE (the opposite bias), 3% below. For comparison the old interval covered 77 to 93% (v2-style) with a median width of 3 ft: expected, the band says what you will see, not an error range.
- **The confidence word did not separate coverage** (high 50% vs low 50%; no medium rows at the tested leads 0/1/3/5 days; leads of 1+ score Hs error only). So it is a judgement, labelled as one, not a validated confidence.
- Per-spot bias correction: NOT applied. The T-090 reconciliation found the spot bias (0.7 to 1.1) unexplained and the forward archive holds only days of data, so nothing supports a per-spot correction yet; the beach differentiation is the sets factor only.
