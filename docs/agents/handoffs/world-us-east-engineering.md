# US East batch 1 in the app (engineering, 2026-11-09)

Branch `world-us-east-app` (from forecast-trust 80b8985). Not deployed: needs a data-honesty review and the user's go. Per-spot table: `docs/forecast/US-EAST-BATCH1.md`.

- **Batch (after review cycle 1: 238):** originally 283 of the 506 spots in `docs/forecast/data/world-spots/us-east.json` (ids in `us-east-batch1-ids.json`): a LIVE NDBC wave buoy within 50 km AND a CO-OPS gauge/prediction station within 25 km, open Atlantic coast only (no Great Lakes, no Gulf). Import: `node --experimental-strip-types --no-warnings docs/forecast/data/add-spots.ts docs/forecast/data/world-spots/us-east.json --ids=docs/forecast/data/world-spots/us-east-batch1-ids.json`.
- **Importer:** `country` words map to ISO codes (full table incl. PR, VI, GU, AS, MP for the Caribbean/Pacific files; an unknown country stops the import); `--ids=<file>` imports a batch list; a row without a `type` takes it from the guide's wave-type words (point/reef only when the guide says so, else Beach); Florida, North Carolina and New Jersey are split into the areas surfers name (by latitude: Northeast Florida / Space Coast / Treasure Coast / Southeast Florida, Outer Banks / Southern North Carolina, Northern / Southern New Jersey).
- **US boxes:** `coverage.ts` US_BOXES gains four Atlantic boxes (Maine-NH coast, Massachusetts to New Jersey, Delaware to Georgia/north Florida, Florida Atlantic east of -82 and west of -79.95 so the Bahamas stay out). The guard test checks every US spot lies inside.
- **Tides:** East picks are reviewed one by one (`EAST_OPEN` list of 50 stations, each with its CO-OPS URL in the override reason; the 14 inlet-mouth stations are `closed-if-alternative`; any other East station id (8xxxxxx / TECnnnn) counts as closed). The word rule alone let through Great South Bay, Barnegat Bay, ICWW, harbours, lakes, Pamlico Sound. Result: 263 spots have a station (a few use a bay-side/inlet one because no open station is within 40 km), 20 have none and say so.
- **NWS zones:** 256 own, 27 borrow a neighbour's within 15 km (inland retry as before). **Shore wind:** only reviewed stations (NDBC_EXPOSURE): Duck Pier, Trident Pier, Sebastian Inlet north jetty (7 spots); Fernandina, Mayport and Port Everglades are recorded as estuary/harbour (never shown). No East ASOS yet: the enclosed-water mask is West Coast only, so an airfield behind a sound would read sound wind.
- **MOP:** none (California only). **Breaking estimates:** profiles from the guides: 172 of 429 spots in total have one.
- **Context buoys:** 24 Atlantic NDBC buoys (every live buoy within 50 km of a batch spot). A spot shows its nearest 4 (MAX_CONTEXT_BUOYS); the Worker serves them on demand only (`onDemand`, the production cron skips them) and the browser loads only the buoys near the scoped spots. No Pacific buoy is within 1,500 km of an East spot.
- **Nav:** zones are states (Maine … Florida), the area as the second level; crumbs read "State › Area".
- **Clock:** the spot page time says "4:00 AM EST"; the Nav badge and the map strip stay PST (Pacific product call).
- **Montauk / Ditch Plains:** Ditch Plains IS in the source file; the batch filter excludes it (nearest buoy 44017 not live, nearest live 44097 is 66.8 km). Only "Montauk Point - Turtles" is missing from the file (no facing). Reason in US-EAST-BATCH1.md.
- **Cron / quota:** hot-only cron, `HOT_MAX` 180: with ALL 429 spots hot the cron counts the newest 180 (7,488 weighted calls/day, peak ~641 subrequests/run, `hot-cron.test.ts` 429/429). Uncapped it would be 17,846/day. Per spot: 3 Open-Meteo targets (marine, weather, spread; 41.6 weighted calls/day).
