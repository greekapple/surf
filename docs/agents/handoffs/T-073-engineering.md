# T-073 handoff (engineering): honest ranges

Full write-up: `docs/forecast/RANGE-T073.md` (method, cited constants, widths before/after, Ocean Beach week as context, cost, limits, review outcome).

- **Method** (`src/services/uncertainty.ts`): range = central height x exp(-w)..exp(+w), w = quadrature sum of: surf-height convention (assumed H1/3..H1/10; Rayleigh ratio 1.27 cited), breaker index 0.60–0.83 (Battjes & Stive 1985; v2 only), offshore-to-nearshore step 0.65–0.97 (our own T-008 comparison; v1 only), wave-model spread (Open-Meteo multi-model, `src/services/modelSpread.ts`), and the v2-vs-v1 method difference. Whole feet, at least 1 ft. "Likely range", not a validated confidence interval. Calibration (T-059) still replaces it when active. Breaker type does not narrow it: no split literature ratios were found, none invented.
- **`headlineSurf` shape is compatible:** same fields; added optional `centralFt` and `interval {halfWidth, components, disagree, note}` on estimates (absent on calibrated ones); `methodLabel` can now add "wave models disagree, so the range is wider" or "fixed 0.7–1.4 band (older cached data)"; new optional ctx fields `spread` (null forces the spread-free range). `FaceEstimate` gained `centralFt`. The Fable agent's scale files can ignore all of it; one-token change in `ScaleStage.tsx`: its `useMemo` for `headlineSurf` now also depends on `spreadV` from `useSwello()` (so it recomputes when a spread arrives), same for `HomeView`'s `ctx`.
- **Calibration:** pairs now carry `basis: 'central'` (the method's central height, also archived as `c`); a fit counts only those, so pairs saved on the old fixed-band midpoint are excluded rather than mixed.
- **Widths** (live, 2026-11-03, 27 of 45 spots with an estimate, 528 daylight spot-hours): median 7 ft before, 5 ft after without the spread, 6 ft with it; narrower in 68%, equal 14%, wider 18%; "wave models disagree" in 38%. v2 spots 6 -> 5, v1 7 -> 6. Ocean Beach week after: 6–11, 8–15, 8–17, 6–14, 9–19, 9–17, 7–15, 11–25 (Surfline's 8–10, 8–12, 8–10, 4–6, 8–12, 8–12, 6–8, 15–18 for context only).
- **Cost:** one extra Open-Meteo request per viewed or saved spot (at most 12), about 16 KB and 0.2 to 1 s, after the main load, cached 10 min, silent on failure; the main forecast load is unchanged.
- **Data-honesty-reviewer** on the diff: no P0; 7 P1 and 5 P2 findings, all addressed (listed in the report).
- **Checks:** tsc, 397 tests (new `uncertainty.test.ts`), build; journeys, t045, t055, t065, t067, t071 and new `t073-range.mjs` pass.
