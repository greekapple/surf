# T-087 handoff (engineering): seabed slopes for more spots
- Method (`docs/forecast/data/mop-slope-all.ts`, reproducible; ETOPO values cached in `etopo-cache.json`, the run aborts on a fetch failure instead of dropping spots): tanβ = MOP point depth / distance to the Natural Earth 10m coastline along the landward shore-normal ray; accepted only if the guide waveType is 'beach', nearest-coast and normal distances agree within 1.5x, tanβ is within 0.004-0.06 (heuristic band, stated as such), and coarse NOAA ETOPO 2022 (15 arc-sec) depth at the point is within 0.4-2.5x of the MOP's depth (a plausibility check, not validation: its cells are as big as the distance to shore). Seven beach-typed spots are excluded by editorial caution (jetty/pier/canyon/rock/steep reflective foreshore: Wedge, Blacks, Carmel, Asilomar, Hermosa, El Porto, Zuma); that list is from general site knowledge, not a published slope source.
- Result: of 40 spots on MOP, 15 are not beach (reef/point/jetty: unknown), 2 fail the geometry check (Crescent City, Morro Rock), 2 are implausible (Moss Landing, Sand Dollar), Linda Mar fails the ETOPO check (the cell is land), 7 are excluded by caution: **12 new + Ocean Beach = 13 of 45 spots now have a breaker type; 32 stay unknown** (5 have no MOP at all). Label: "mean slope from the CDIP point depth and the distance to shore; approximate, a model point at depth, not the slope at the break".
- Safety: generated slopes carry `confidence: 'mean-slope'`; only a hand-checked slope (`'checked'`, Ocean Beach) may downgrade a verdict (the "plunging at size = Skip for non-experts" rule). Mean-slope spots get a soft note ("likely hollow and powerful" / "likely soft, but check the shorebreak"), the 3D wave family, and the Board Match character.
- Before/after, 13 spots x next 72 h (949 classified spot-hours; before: 73 hours, all Ocean Beach): after = spilling 917, plunging 32, surging 0. Against the old (mis-applied) thresholds, 20 spot-hours move plunging -> spilling (the T-085 fix). t062-live, t045 and journeys pass; I did not eyeball new-spot 3D screenshots.
- Limits: a mean slope over the shoaling zone understates the foreshore slope on steep or barred beaches, so "spilling" may be too soft there; that is why verdict downgrades are limited to hand-checked slopes.
- Checks: tsc, 431 tests, build, journeys 8/8, t045/t055/t062/t065/t073/t074 pass. Reviewer: no P0; P1s fixed (confidence gate, exclusions, "plausibility check" wording, reproducible cache, thresholds stated as heuristics).
