# T-033 Engineering handoff: AI wave-scale bands replace the 3D wave

**Branch:** `worktree-engineering` (synced with `rebuild/swello-v0.16` first). Owned: `src/features/scale/*`, the Forecast wave card, manifest entries, plus small `src/ui/Media*` additions.

## What changed
- **`src/services/waveScale.ts` (new, pure, tested):** 8 bands `1–2, 2–3, 3–4, 4–6, 6–8, 8–10, 10–15, 15+` (lower edge inclusive: exactly 2 ft is the 2–3 band; anything under 2 ft, including under 1 ft, uses 1–2 and says so; 15+ is open ended). `selectScaleBand` picks the band for the hour: the presentable breaking estimate first (label "Est. 4–6 ft at the break"), else the demonstration face ("6 ft demonstration face, not live data"), else offshore Hs ("4.1 ft offshore, not surf size", plus a note that offshore is not the size of the breaking wave), else no band ("No wave height for this hour", not tied to the forecast). A range spanning two or more bands uses the band of its **midpoint** and says so ("The estimate spans the 3–4 ft and 4–6 ft bands. Showing the band of its midpoint (4 ft)."); a range that ends exactly on a band edge ("4–6") is one band.
- **`WaveBands.tsx` (new):** the illustration for that band, the caption "Illustration · AI-generated, approximate scale, not current conditions" on every image, a band badge, the labelled source line, and a radiogroup of all 8 bands (click or arrow keys; "Back to this hour" when browsing; the hour's own band is marked with an accent underline). Phones get stills; the loop plays at ≥ 861 px on the full page, and on tap ("Play loop") on phones and in the compact Forecast card (so a Forecast page never has two videos). Reduced motion: still only, no loop button.
- **Wave Scale page** (`/forecast/wave-scale`): now this component full size plus "Back to … forecast"; the 2D Wave comparison stays below. **Forecast wave card:** the same component, compact, with "Open full scale". The hero "Wave scale" button (first phone screen) opens the page, so the scale is one tap from the first screen.
- **3D removed:** deleted `scene.ts`, `wave.ts`, `waveShader.ts`, `MiniScale.tsx` and the 3D CSS. Nothing else used them; `three` is still needed by the Quiver showroom (`quiver/studio.ts`), so the dependency stays. Route label is now "Wave scale".
- **Media:** `AI_CAPTIONS` allows the two sanctioned captions only (`captionFor` can never show anything else); new `still` prop and `useMediaEntry` hook. Manifest: `ai-scale-{1-2,2-3,3-4,4-6,6-8,8-10,10-15,15-plus}ft` with 900/1600 stills, a loop on the four bands that have one, a CC fallback photo each.
