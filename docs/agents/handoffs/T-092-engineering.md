# T-092 handoff (engineering): map raster ocean mask
- Problem (design critic): the sea-state raster painted over SF Bay and left a coloured fringe along land edges.
- `docs/forecast/data/enclosed-waters.ts` derives ENCLOSED waters from the repo's Natural Earth 10m coastline (no hand-drawn polygons): on a 0.01 degree grid, an open core = water at least 3 km from land and connected to the open Pacific; open = within 3.6 km of the core; enclosed = the rest (so a bay behind a narrow mouth such as the Golden Gate is disconnected), grown by one cell over the shoreline. Output `src/map/enclosed-waters.json` (5 KB), read by `src/map/enclosed.ts`. R_OPEN and R_DILATE are stated heuristics; `src/tests/enclosed-waters.test.ts` pins named points both ways (SF Bay central/south/San Pablo/Richardson Bay and Humboldt Bay enclosed; Golden Gate ocean side, Ocean Beach, Half Moon Bay, Monterey Bay centre and head, Drakes Bay, Santa Monica Bay, 50 km offshore open).
- `src/map/field.ts`: enclosed points return no value for the raster, arrows and flow; a light hatch marks them and a quiet "Not covered" label (drawn after the land trim, with a halo) appears once per large area; a 3 px coast stroke is trimmed along the land edge to remove the colour fringe. The legend chip's source popover adds: "Enclosed waters (bays, harbours, estuaries) are left blank: the wave-model grid does not resolve them."
- Limits: small bays that the 1:10m coastline draws as land (Morro Bay, San Diego Bay, Mission Bay, Tomales Bay) were already trimmed as land, so they show no colour either, but no "Not covered" hatch (they are not in the water mask). Spot pins and readings are unaffected (display only).
