## Surfline (Central Ocean Beach spot page, captured live 2026-10-31 3 pm)

Anatomy, top to bottom: ad banner → global bar (Cams & Forecasts · Maps · Learn · Watch · About, search, Get App, Go Premium) → favourites strip with condition colour + height → spot title + "Nearby spots" + "Add favorite" → tabs (Report & Forecast · Analysis · Charts · Guide) → **multi-cam** (live player + 4 cam thumbnails, single/grid toggle, time-of-clip picker) → **Nearby spots** row (cam still, height, OBSERVED tag) → **Current Surf Conditions** card grid → forecaster text → **Forecast**: 10-day day strip (height range, 3 wind arrows per day, 3-segment AM/mid/PM rating bar, starred "days to watch") → per-day forecaster note → **Cam Matches** (past cam clips whose forecast matched, with % match) → hourly **rating bar** → surf-height bar chart (forecast bars + forecaster observation pins) → wind bars (speed + gust, GFS) → **tide** curve with first light/sunrise/sunset/last light → **swell** components plot → **energy** (nearshore vs offshore kJ, % swell vs windsea) → **consistency** /100 → weather → model run time → **nearby buoys** (each: height, period, direction + individual swell partitions) → spot guide prose → breadcrumb.

## Windy (windy.com, captured live 2026-10-31 at 1440 and 390, logged out, Ocean Beach SF)

Anatomy: full-bleed map with animated particles (wind) or drifting crest glyphs (waves) over a colour field → **layer list** on the right (radar, satellite, wind, rain, temperature, clouds, **Waves**, More layers: swell 1/2/3, wind waves, period, currents…) → **timeline scrubber** along the bottom (day ticks, an hour bubble "3 PM", play button; days past the free range marked premium) → **colour legend** bar bottom-right with the unit inside it (tap cycles ft/m, kt/mph) → **model chips** beside it (ECMWF WAM 9 km · RDWPS 2.5 km · "1 more") → tap a point: **swell rose on the map** (a ring at the point with one coloured arrow per swell, labelled "3 ft, 13 s", "2 ft, 7 s") plus a **bottom forecast panel**: an hourly table (hours, icons, temp, **wind + gust in speed-coloured cells with arrows**, waves, swell 1, swell period, **wave power kW/m**, tides (premium), water temp) with tabs Basic · **Waves & Tides** · Meteogram · Wind · Airgram, "Source: ECMWF WAM" picker and "Settings for location forecast" → **Compare forecasts** (multimodel): one row per model (meteoblue AI, ECMWF, GFS, ICON, HRRR…), each with its own update time, hide/pin. Also: nearest weather station bubble, webcams in vicinity, particles/pressure toggles, 3D globe. Phone: search bar with current temp + 4-day mini forecast, map, floating buttons, compact timeline with the legend under it; the point forecast is a bottom sheet with a horizontally scrolling table and a bar for interval (3 h), "Display as Wave&Tide" and source. Screenshots: `windy/1440-0x-*.png`, `windy/390-0x-*.png`, `windy/1440-07b-model-picker.png`, `windy/1440-07c-model-compare.png`.

**What Windy teaches us:** one shared time scrubber, legends with units on every colour scale, the swell rose at the point, and open model comparison as a confidence signal. Its weakness for surfers: no surf height, no rating, no shore relation; it shows the ocean, not the break. That gap is ours (spot-specific rating, labelled breaking estimate, wind relative to the beach).

## What SWELLO adds that none of them have
1. **Spot-specific rating that explains itself** (window, period, tide, wind, bottom) — T-040.
2. **Forecast vs buoy "now" accuracy** shown openly.
3. **3D wave-vs-surfer scale** at the forecast height (Blender renders, licensed wave model + to-scale surfer).
4. **Board Match from your own quiver and ability**, with the board room.
5. Calm, minimal, no ads, no AI imagery on condition surfaces.
