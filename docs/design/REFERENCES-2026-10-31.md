## 1. Porsche 911 configurator → the Quiver "board room" (user: "the Porsche configurator is for the board room")
URL: configurator.porsche.com (911 Carrera GTS, 3D mode). Captured 1440 and 390.

Borrow:
- **One subject, big, on a light set.** The car fills ~65% of the width at 1440 on a bright, real-looking set (white wall, paving, soft shadow). Nothing else competes.
- **Stage controls inside the stage, bottom-left pill:** Adjust scene · Compare · 360° View · download · fullscreen. Hotspots (small white rings) sit on the subject and open detail.
- **Camera presets as a thumbnail strip** under the stage (10 angles incl. interior); the selected one is outlined. On phones it collapses to a "5 / 10" counter on the stage with swipe.
- **Options panel on the right (1440), below the stage (390):** grouped sections (Exterior Colors → Contrasts / Shades / Dreams / Legends …), large real-material swatches, the selected one outlined with its name shown ("Guards Red"), the price delta next to each group. A search field above the options.
- **Phone layout:** compact title row (back · model · year), stage pinned at the top while options scroll under it, **sticky bottom bar** with the running total and one primary action.
- Typography is small and calm: 16–17 px body, section titles ~20 px, model name ~32 px. Big type is not what makes it premium; the subject and the set are.

SWELLO mapping (board room): stage = the user's board (real outline/scan, or the type illustration) on a light set; hotspots on the board = fins, tail, rails, nose; option groups = Dimensions (L/W/T/V), Fins, Tail, Construction, Colour; the sticky bar = Board Match for the selected spot/day + "Use for today"; thumbnails = deck / bottom / rail / tail / nose / 3-4 views; "Compare" = two boards side by side.

Do not copy: Porsche branding, car imagery, prices, the dealer flow.

## 3. Phone density rule (user, 2026-10-30: "many things are crowded and oversized on the phone")
At 390: body 15–16 px, labels 12–13 px, section titles ≤ 22 px, page titles ≤ 30 px, the one hero number ≤ 56 px. Minimum 16 px gutters, 12 px between controls, one primary action per screen. Measured by script, not eyeballed.
