# Phone critique, 2026-11-10 (390 px, iPhone 13 emulation)

Screens: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-milosin-Development-projects-surf/250e1e66-bb9b-4659-a5ff-86bd5a0084e7/scratchpad/phone/` (home, spot = Ocean Beach, spot-hi = Hookipa, map, map-out, quiver, personal). Screenshots are 3x, so the visible viewport is 390x664 CSS px; all positions below are CSS px (screenshot px / 3). Header and tab-bar sizes were measured in Chrome at 390x664 (`~/Development/ai-system-setup/playwright-toolkit/swello/_phone-critique-measure.mjs`).
References: `docs/design/RULES.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `swello-design-references/15-surfline-nav/windy-390-map.png`, `surfline-390-home.png`, `03-explore/windy-mobile.jpg`. (`17-surfline-charts/390-01-norcal.png` is a Cloudflare bot-check page, so there is no Surfline phone chart to compare against.)

**Verdict: Partly.** The honesty labels, the rating colours and the tab bar are sound. The phone layouts, though, are the desktop layout shrunk to fit: identity is repeated, two-column blocks are squeezed into 170 px, labels collide, and the map's controls take ~45% of the screen. Surfline puts the answer on the first screen and Windy gives the map the whole screen. Neither is true on the phone yet.

## Ranked problems (by user impact)

1. **Spot, first screen: the answer comes last.** (spot-top) The hero photo takes 184 px (y 124–308) and holds only the name, a 2-line qualifier, the time and the photo credit. Below it come the advisory (57 px) and a 4-column light grid. "10–13 ft" finally appears at y≈470, and wind, tide and rating are below the fold. A surfer in the parking lot scrolls before learning anything. *Fix:* delete the selector row and make the H1 the selector (tap the name to open the spot sheet). Put the number in the hero: surf range in expanded Archivo at about 56 px, with "model estimate · high confidence" as one 13 px line directly under it, then the rating bar and a single "Wind 3 mph N cross-shore · Tide 5.8 ft falling" line. Shrink the credit to a 44 px "(i)" in the photo corner that opens the credit. Keep the honesty line "heights are model estimates · ratings provisional", but move it next to the number rather than under the name.

2. **Map: the controls take half the screen.** (map-top, map-out) The app header (63 px) stays on top of a full-bleed map. Under the in-map search come the selected-spot chip and two 44 px FABs. Along the bottom are a full-width attribution bar, a translucent time bar (basemap labels "Salinas" and "Green…" show through it) and a separate legend card with only 3 ticks. Windy (windy-390-map.png) runs the map edge to edge, with a thin time bar and a 20 px colour strip as its bottom edge. *Fix:* hide the app header on /explore (the map's own search replaces it). Use the compact MapLibre attribution (`attributionControl:{compact:true}`, an "(i)" at bottom-left). Make the time bar opaque at 44 px. Join the legend to the time bar's bottom edge as a 16 px strip with ticks 0 1.6 3.3 5 6.6 10 20 30+, and keep "Offshore wave height · not surf size" as the strip's left label (spell it out; "Hs" is jargon). This frees about 110 px of map.

3. **Map: pin labels and basemap labels collide; region cards spill out of their boxes.** (map-top y 470–960; map-out x 140–840, y 380–1160 at 3x) Pins are DOM markers, so MapLibre's collision engine never sees them. In the zoomed-out view, the "striped = forecast 13 h old" text and its hatched bar run ~8 px past the right edge of three region cards. The same legend sentence is repeated three times, and the cards cover the coastline they describe. *Fix:* render the pin value labels as a symbol layer (or give place labels `text-optional` with a lower `symbol-sort-key`) so place names give way to pins. Dim place labels to 60% opacity over the sea field. Shrink each region card to a pill ("North Coast · Solid", hatched when stale, with a leader to the coast) and state "striped = forecast 13 h old" once, in the legend.

4. **Map zoomed out: the sea renders black.** (map-out, whole ocean) The legend's calm colour is navy, but the sea shows black with white streaks, so no colour on screen matches the legend. Either the field hadn't loaded at this zoom or it doesn't render there. If it is a loading state, it needs a skeleton or a "loading field" note. If the field doesn't exist at this zoom, the legend must say so. Showing a legend over an unpainted sea suggests a reading that isn't there. *Fix:* check the field at z≤5. Until it renders, show the legend as "field loads when you zoom in" and grey out the strip.

5. **Hookipa hero (no photo): the illustration overlaps the text.** (spot-hi-top, x 180–360, y 185–245) The wave and figure draw over "10:00 AM HST · now" and sit under the favourite star, and the caption "Drawn from the forecast · approximate" overlaps the star's touch area. *Fix:* when there is no photo, give the hero a two-row grid (text row, then an illustration row with reserved height). Better, drop the illustration from the hero, because the Wave scale section lower down already draws the same figure to scale.

6. **Spot charts: labels collide and one axis carries no information.** (spot-1 top: tide labels "12:39AM5:02AM L" overprint; spot-2 y≈1200–1250 at 3x: "Tue" / "Surf" / the now-cursor overlap; "Swell" and "Wind" labels are cut by the cursor; spot-3: "12 AM" repeated 7 times on the x axis) 7 days in 340 px gives 48 px per day, so the extra labels just add noise. *Fix:* put the panel titles in the left y-axis gutter above the axis numbers, never in the plot area. Drop the x-axis in the 7-day view, since the day bands at the top already carry it, and show "6a 12p 6p" only in the 1-day view. On the tide mini chart, drop any label within 40 px of another and keep only the H/L heights.

7. **Two-column blocks at 390 px force ugly wraps.** (spot-hi-top y 500–600; spot-1; spot-hi-1) Wind/Tide and Rating/Water each get ~170 px per column, which produces "gusts 37.4 / mph", "High Tue 1:43 PM · 1.4 / ft", a three-line station note and "Wetsuit: common surf-shop / guide". *Fix:* below 480 px, switch to one column of rows: label on the left, number right-aligned in tabular figures, and a single note line below (or the note behind the existing chevron). Rating becomes a full-width row: word, bar, then the 3 factor dots on one line.

8. **The day strip repeats itself and hides the scroll.** (spot-2 y 340–700) Every day column repeats "est. · high confidence" over two lines. The fifth day is cut off at the edge with no scroll cue. Below the strip is a 4-line confidence paragraph ("0 h ahead, 2 wave models agree; … ±17% …"). *Fix:* put one line under the strip: "est. · high confidence to Fri; hatched = less certain". Mark each day's confidence by opacity or hatching only. Add a 24 px fade on the right edge. Move the confidence paragraph behind a "Why this confidence" disclosure. RULES says: "One short honest note per data source, placed where the number is."

9. **Grey qualifier lines and copy errors around the numbers.** (spot-top y 490–600; spot-2 top) Under "10–13 ft" sit "Overhead to double overhead surf height", "est. breaker type…", "model estimate · high confidence", "How right we've been: no buoy can check this spot this month." and "CDIP's model … could not be fetched today." The board paragraph has copy errors: "…9 s period, A 9.2 s swell is short…" (capital A mid-sentence) and "expect it to feel soft.." (double full stop). *Fix:* keep "model estimate · high confidence" on the number's baseline. Keep the overhead descriptor. Move "How right we've been" and the CDIP fetch note into Sources & data, with a small "(i) accuracy" link beside the number. Fix the board copy, and lead with a one-line verdict ("Bigger-day option, 6'6"; swell short for it, will feel soft") with the reasoning behind "Board Match".

10. **Header on phone: crowded, and one control is duplicated.** (all *-top) The wordmark (93 px), search (128 px, placeholder only just fits), an unlabelled status dot, the theme toggle and the avatar share 390 px. The theme setting already exists under Personal > Appearance. On the Personal page a stray ~32x2 px dark underline sits under the avatar (personal-top x 342–374, y 64), which looks like a desktop active-tab indicator showing through. *Fix:* on phone, show the mark (icon only), a search field filling the remaining width, and the avatar. Remove the theme toggle on phone. Turn the status dot into a small badge on the avatar, or an "Updated 12:04" line in the page. Hide the active underline below 480 px.

11. **Home: the hero is mostly illustration, and the table is explained in a paragraph.** (home-top; home-1) The answer's numbers are buried in a sentence. The illustration takes ~140 px (y 315–455) and its caption repeats the sentence. "This week" opens with a 4-line paragraph explaining the table, which RULES lists under "Do not". The table clips at "F…" with no scroll cue, and the spot column is 115 px with names wrapping to 2–3 lines ("Sloat (Ocean / Beach south)"). The Popular spots section is a stack of identical cards, each with a "Next 12 hours +" row. *Fix:* hero leads with "Kelly's Cove · 12–2 PM" and a large "6–8 ft" with its label, then the board line, then the CTA. Cut the illustration to ~90 px or to a 44 px thumbnail that links to Wave scale. Replace the paragraph with a one-line key (rating dashes = AM·mid·PM, hatch = less certain, line = midday trend). Make the first column sticky at 96 px with an edge fade. Make Popular spots a ruled list, not cards.

12. **Quiver stage: the fit line collides with the board, and the board is small.** (quiver-top y 190–330) "Size fit for est. 10–13 ft at Ocean Beach" floats inside the stage beside the ruler and almost touches the board's nose. A 5'11" board fills about a quarter of a stage ruled to 8'. The stage uses three different grounds (white, warm grey, beige thumbnails). *Fix:* move the fit line under the board name, next to "Surf above its range". Scale the ruler to the longest board in the quiver plus 1', so the board fills ~70% of the stage height. Use one ground colour across the stage and thumbnails.

## Three signature improvements (premium, not generic)

1. **A one-glance hero you can swipe between favourites.** On the spot page and Home, the hero is a single number block over the credited photo: the surf range in expanded Archivo at ~64 px, the rating bar, and a wind arrow drawn against this beach's shoreline so on-, cross- or offshore reads as a shape rather than a word, plus a tide arrow. Swipe sideways to page through favourite spots with paging dots, like the iOS Weather app's cities. Honesty labels stay as one 13 px line under the number. Surfline needs a cam and a premium wall for this read; Swello can give it from data in one second.

2. **A map bottom sheet that scrubs time.** Tap a pin and a 96 px sheet peeks up with the spot's estimate, rating and next good window. Drag it up for the 12-hour strip. While the time bar is scrubbed, every pin's number and colour update live, snapping to each hour (with `navigator.vibrate` where the browser supports it). The time bar and legend are a single bottom element, Windy-style. This combines Windy's scrubbing with Surfline's spot read, and neither app offers it on a phone.

3. **A "Today ribbon".** One horizontally scrolling band, ~88 px tall, that replaces the 4-column light grid and the Wind/Tide/Water columns. Along an hour axis it shows the daylight band (first light to last light shaded), the surf estimate as a filled line, wind arrows relative to the shoreline, and the tide curve, with the best window bracketed in the accent colour and labelled "Best 12–2 PM". One thumb-scroll answers "when". Every track keeps its label (model estimate, NOAA station name and distance) in the ribbon's left gutter.

## What works (don't regress)
- Honest labels sit next to the numbers ("model estimate", "Offshore … not surf", "NOAA station prediction, not at the beach"), and the rating colour scale is the same in every view.
- The tab bar: 5 even 78x63 targets, a single accent for state, clear labels.
- Personal's ruled-list rhythm (label left, value right, chevron) is the pattern items 7 and 11 should copy.

## Not checked
Dark theme. Interaction and motion (scrub latency, sheet behaviour, pressed states). Real iOS Safari toolbars and safe-area insets. Landscape. Home with favourites set. Intermediate map zooms. Whether the black ocean at zoom-out (item 4) is a timing artefact of the capture.
