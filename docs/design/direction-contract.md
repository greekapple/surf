# Direction contract — SURF app (Phase 9C)
Development-only. Never copy into source, markup, comments or served files.

Scope: whole app. Modes: Forecast and Explore = Operate; Quiver and Scale = Experience inside the same world.

**THESIS** — A verdict board, not a dashboard: one signal answers "worth it, when, why" for one break, and every number underneath carries its source and age. Refuses the category default (dark navy/cyan multi-widget dashboard) and the prototype's cream-and-serif editorial page.

**OWN-WORLD** — Ocean Beach fog and Golden Gate signage. Fog-white ground, charcoal and black panels, a single International Orange signal reserved for the verdict and "now"; coastal blue only for water data. Archivo set wide and heavy for signage-scale words and numerals, narrow for dense numerals; system text for prose. Square corners, hairline rules, solid fills; provenance as small, consistent tags (unit · source · age), the way a nautical chart notes soundings. Boards drawn as true-proportion dimensioned outlines.

**STORY** — The surfer sees the break and its size range instantly, reads the verdict and its three reasons (swell, wind, tide), drags one time rail to see when it improves, and can open the evidence behind any value. Uncertainty lowers the signal's intensity rather than hiding in footnotes.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Forecast: spot name in wide caps top-left with region and local time; the size range is the largest object on screen with its quantity label beneath (offshore Hs / face n/a when not estimated); verdict line with orange signal block and reliability marker; three reason rows; the time rail at the bottom edge, thumb-reachable, synchronised with charts and map. Primary navigation: bottom bar on phones, top bar on desktop.

**FORM** — Signage verdict board, first of three hand-derived candidates (Fog & Signal; alternates Chart Room, Shaping Bay). seed: none (unavailable — Impeccable launcher not installed).

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
