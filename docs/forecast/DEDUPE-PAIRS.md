# Same-break pairs (dedupe-pairs, 2026-11-11)

Rule: the same guide page(s) = the same break = one row. Slug-suffix twins merge only when the names match after normalisation (lowercase, parentheticals and punctuation out, street→st, words state/beach/the/st dropped), the **guides' own pins** (surfnerd page coordinates, read 2026-11-11 at 1 request/s) are within 300 m, and the stated facings are equal. Two different guide pages for different sections stay two spots with the guides' own names. Unclear stays unchanged. `scripts/dedupe-pairs.py` records the decision; `src/tests/dedupe-pairs.test.ts` keeps it true for the whole catalogue.

Counts: 1,081 spots before, 1,078 after (three merges).

- **Merged** (same break, names equal, rows 100-150 m apart, equal facing): Avalon Pier NC (`w-us-avalon-pier-north-carolina` into `w-us-avalon-pier`), Flagler (`w-us-flagler-pier` into `w-us-flagler-beach-pier`), A St. (`w-us-a-st` into `w-us-a-street`). The merged-away rows are in `dropped` of `world-spots/us-east.json` (dropReason). Their ids stay as aliases (`src/data/spotAliases.ts`) for favourites, imports, sessions, felt entries, the archive, recents, alert rules and `?spot=`. **The kept row's facts are not changed:** surfnerd's two pages for each break give different swell windows, so the other page is NOT added to `sources`; the kept row's `notes` says "surfnerd also lists this break at <url> (row …, merged into this one): its page gives the swell window … which differs from this page's."
- **Not merged, Walton Rocks:** surfnerd has two different pages, `walton-rocks` (a sand beach break) and `walton-rocks-2` (a reef, "fickle … powerful left"), 150 m apart. Both kept with their own facts; the catalogue test skips pairs whose guide bottoms differ.
- **Renamed from the guides' own words:** Trails (San Onofre State Beach), Point Dume / Little Dume, Haggertys and Malaga Cove (surf-forecast.com's heading; the namesake "Haggerty's" reef row exists, so the apostrophe form would repeat a name). Old bare slugs `trails` and `point-dume` now reach the namesake spots `Trails` / `Point Dume` (unique names in the one slug index, `src/app/slugs.ts`); no alias takes a live spot's slug (a test).
- **Region fixed:** Abalone Cove Beach, Palm Point, Royal Palms (the Jetty), Cabrillo Point are Los Angeles (were Orange County).
- **Distinct breaks sharing a guide page (decision, lead 2026-11-11): kept separate:** Davenport / Davenport Landing (2.2 km by our rows), Crescent City / South Beach (1.0 km), Morro Rock / Morro Strand (2.1 km). The guides' pins are identical for Morro and Davenport.
- **Identical names, guide pins read, none within 300 m, left unchanged:** Egypt Beach MA (pins 4.3 km apart), Fire Island NY (26.5 km, facings differ), Lido Beach NY (1.06 km), Jones Beach State Park NY (13.9 km), Lavallette NJ (1.65 km), Island Beach State Park NJ (4.6 km, facings differ), Camp Pendleton VA (3.3 km, facings differ), Middles PR (1.7 km, facings differ).
- **Left out of this branch:** the importer's own break-type changes for Onslow Beach, Cocoa Beach and 2nd Light (null to Beach) were reverted; they need their pages cited.
