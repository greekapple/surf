**Display.** Under the amended camera rule (PRODUCT.md): display is OK for public domain, explicit embed terms, or an owner-enabled YouTube stream; credit it, load on tap, state live status honestly; AI analysis still needs written permission or public domain. The usable-now cameras of `docs/forecast/BAYAREA-CAMERAS.md` (rows in `camera-sources.json`), shown on the spot (forecast) page between the daylight row and the conditions. **The still.** Loaded by the visitor's browser from USGS's own host. Its time is the owner's `Last-Modified` header, which the browser cannot read (the bucket sends no CORS headers), so the Worker's `/api/cam-meta?id=usgs-dream-inn-c2` (`services/cam-meta.mjs`) makes one HEAD request to the fixed URL and returns only the time (kept 5 minutes; 60 s after a miss; an unknown id is a 404; no image is fetched or stored). **Age (audit A1).** The file carries no capture time (no EXIF, no JPEG comment; the stamp "Tue 11/10/2026 17:30 PST" and the epoch file name "F: 1794360600.c2.snap.jpg" are only in the picture's pixels, which the Worker does not decode). `Last-Modified` is a REWRITE time: the same 17:30 frame was re-posted at 19:13 and at 23:13 on 2026-11-10, and by day a frame was posted 13–31 min after its stamp. So the capture time is bounded, not guessed: taken is at most min(posted, when the Worker first saw this content (its ETag, `/api/cam-meta` `firstSeenAt`)), and because USGS collects in daylight only, at most the most recent last light before that bound (the previous evening's when the bound is before first light). The still is Offline once that bound is more than 2 h old. By day the caption reads "Frame posted 9:43 AM PST (7 min ago); the picture was taken a little earlier…"; a re-posted frame reads "Picture taken about 5:31 PM PST or earlier (…, an estimate): USGS collects in daylight only, and this frame was re-posted 11:13 PM PST". No constant lag is claimed.

**The streams.** A lite embed: our own poster (never YouTube's thumbnail: the Chamberlin thumbnails are a 2020 storm photo), titled "Video stream · <owner>" with "Live when we last checked it by hand (Nov 10, 2026). Tap to load YouTube's player; it shows whether the stream is live now." Nothing is requested from YouTube, ytimg or googlevideo until the visitor taps; then the IFrame API loads and the player autoplays. The hand check was a page read plus a headless embed on 2026-11-10 (Linda Mar re-checked the same day through YouTube's oEmbed: HTTP 200, author "Linda Mar Beach Cam", embedding on, operator still unnamed; recorded in the row's `recheck`); **SWELLO does not monitor the streams**, and does not probe YouTube itself (its terms forbid automated access). So **"Offline" for a stream comes from the player after the tap**: an error code (100 removed or private; 101 and 150 embedding turned off by the owner; 153 "the YouTube player could not verify this site (a SWELLO setup problem, not the camera)"), the player's own `isLive` = false (the player is paused and dimmed: "a recording or an ended broadcast"), the end of the stream, or no player within 12 s ("could not load"). Credits: "Chamberlin Nature (pacificaview.net), live cam via YouTube" with links to the stream and the channel; "Linda Mar Beach Cam, live cam via YouTube" (the row publishes "Live cam: Linda Mar Beach Cam via YouTube") with its links.

**Checks.** `src/tests/live-cams.test.ts` (data = generator output, only the four spots have cameras, each row usable now and the distances and URLs are the row's, credits, the 2 h rule at its edge, age and time words, stream words, the Worker endpoint, no YouTube iframe/thumbnail host/API before a click, no storage or canvas, no panel in Home or the map); browser: `playwright-toolkit/swello/live-cam.mjs` at 1440 and 390 (the still loads, the time and age in PST, the credit, no YouTube request before a tap and the player after it, another view is a fresh poster, no panel on Ocean Beach, Rockaway, Home or the map, the Offline paths with the frame hidden and a 1 h 55 min frame still shown).

## HDOnTap cameras (hdontap-embeds, hdontap-auto, hdontap-licence; 2026-11-10)
**Basis: "HDOnTap: embedding needs a paid licence (HDOnTap email, 2026-11-10); link only".** HDOnTap's support replied (lead #10098) that it does **not** offer free third-party embedding and will **not** whitelist external domains: embedding is paid licensing only, a monthly content licence plus a streaming fee, per camera. An earlier note that HDOnTap "agreed" was a misunderstanding. Nothing on the site claims HDOnTap's permission.

**All 16 HDOnTap embeds are disabled** (`enabled: false`, `disabledReason: "HDOnTap requires a paid licence for embedding (email 2026-11-10)"`), so the Camera panel never draws one. Each camera keeps its "HDOnTap cam ↗" link (HdontapLinks): 24 links for the HDOnTap rows within about 5 km of an app spot whose view shows the break or the beach. Their player pages also still send `frame-ancestors 'self' https://hdontap.com https://*.hdontap.com`, so a frame would be refused by the browser anyway.

**Plumbing kept for a bought licence.** `services/embed-allowed.mjs` (`GET /api/embed-allowed?id=<player id>`: one HEAD to the camera's own player page, parses `Content-Security-Policy` `frame-ancestors` and `X-Frame-Options`, `allowed: true` only when `https://swello.surf-prototype.workers.dev` matches; cached 10 minutes when allowed, 60 seconds when not) and the page hook `useEmbedAllowed` stay in the code. If a licence is ever bought for a camera: add its row id to `HDONTAP_ENABLED` in `scripts/build-live-cams.mjs`, rerun the generator, and the panel will draw that camera only when HDOnTap's headers allow our origin, with a credit that names the licence. Until then nothing in the panel depends on it.
- The raw HLS (m3u8) and any proxy are never used.

## The kill switch (one line per camera)
Every camera in the panel has `enabled` in `src/data/liveCamsSourced.ts`, generated by `scripts/build-live-cams.mjs`.
- **Switch one off** (a removal request, or a camera that misbehaves): add one line to `DISABLED` at the top of the script, `'<row id>@<spot id>': 'the reason'`, then `node scripts/build-live-cams.mjs > src/data/liveCamsSourced.ts` and deploy. The panel then renders nothing for it (and the spot page for a spot with no enabled camera shows no panel).
- **Switch an HDOnTap embed on** (once HDOnTap has approved our domain for that stream): add the row id to `HDONTAP_ENABLED` in the same script, rerun the same command, check the player loads on the live site, deploy.
- Tests (`src/tests/hdontap-cams.test.ts`, `embed-allowed.test.ts`) fail if a disabled camera could render an iframe, or if the panel could draw an HDOnTap camera without the Worker's confirmation.
