## 1. Wave buoys: `IWBNetwork` (`fetchMiBuoys`, `normalizeMiBuoys`)
- Request (one for every station): `https://erddap.marine.ie/erddap/tabledap/IWBNetwork.csv?station_id,longitude,latitude,time,WaveHeight,WavePeriod,MeanWaveDirection,Hmax,Tp,ThTp,SprTp,QC_Flag&time>=<now-24h, floored to the hour>`. CSV: line 1 names, **line 2 units (skipped)**; `NaN` and blanks are null, never 0.
- Stations: the dataset lists M1, M2, M3, M4, M4-Archive, M5, M6, FS1 and Belmullet-AMETS; only **M2, M3, M5, M6 are live** (newest 2026-11-10 08:00Z, lag about 1.3 h). M4's newest row is 2026-03-29T04:00Z, so the 24 h filter drops it and the reader reports it in `silent` with its last row. AMETS (Belmullet) is silent since 2011 here (the study found the wave-buoy datasets silent since 2026-05-27).
- Output, per live buoy: `{id, lat, lon, validAt, ageMinutes, obs: ObservationSet}` where `obs.values` uses the context-buoy names of `buoys.ts`: **"Offshore significant height"** (`WaveHeight`, m converted to ft, `nativeUnit` "m"), **"Dominant wave period"** (`Tp`, s) and **"Peak wave direction"** (`ThTp`, degrees true). Each variable takes its own newest valid row (no filling across hours). The buoy's place is its own position ("M3 weather buoy (51.22° N, 10.55° W), Irish Weather Buoy Network"): no place name is invented.
- Rules: a row counts only within 24 h of the retrieval, not in the future (5 min grace), and `QC_Flag` not 9. **QC_Flag values in this dataset: 0 "unknown", 1 "good", 9 "missing_value"** (SeaDataNet). Every live row is flagged 0, so the notes say "quality flag 0 (unknown: the dataset does not qualify it)"; flag 1 would read "good". `WaveHeight` is the significant height (4 x RMS, 17.5-minute record). No swell split: the buoy is context ("measured offshore"), never the spot's surf; the notes say so.
- **To confirm with the Marine Institute:** the direction. `ThTp`'s `long_name` reads "Directional spreading at the spectral peak period" while its `comment` says "Mean wave direction at spectral peak period (Tp)"; the values (M3 196.9° in a southwesterly swell) are directions, and `MeanWaveDirection` is a different, mean direction. The reader labels `ThTp` "Peak wave direction" as the study did.
- Recorded fixture: `iwbnetwork-recorded.csv` (the 2026-11-10 09:25Z response for the last 8 h, plus M4's last row spliced in so the silent-station case is tested). Live result at 09:3x UTC: M2 1.17 m, M3 3.63 m, M5 3.16 m, M6 4.34 m, all 87 minutes old.

## 2. Tide predictions: `IMI_TidePrediction_HighLow` (`fetchMiTides`, `normalizeMiTides`, `MI_TIDE_STATIONS`)
- Request, one station: `https://erddap.marine.ie/erddap/tabledap/IMI_TidePrediction_HighLow.csv?stationID,time,tide_time_category,Water_Level_ODMalin&stationID="<id>"&time>=<start>&time<=<end>` (the study's form; `fetchMiTides` asks for the UTC day and the next one).
- **38 stations**, ids as the dataset spells them (`Lahinch`, `Malin_Head`, `Port_Oriel`, `Dublin_Port`...), with the dataset's own positions in `MI_TIDE_STATIONS` (equal to the recorded list, tested). Station coverage of Irish spots (every spot within 42.6 km of one, median 13.3 km) is in EUROPE-DATA.md.
- Output: `MiTides {stationId, stationName, zone: "Europe/Dublin", datum: "OD Malin", datumNote, attribution, extrema: TidePrediction[]}`; each extremum `{timestamp (ISO UTC), type "High"|"Low", height: Measurement}` is the shape of the tide card's NOAA extrema. Heights are converted to ft like the NOAA ones, with `nativeUnit` "m OD Malin", location "Irish Marine Institute prediction at <station>" (the label the study asks for, also for Northern Ireland spots served by Malin Head or Port Oriel, which must say "Irish prediction station"), and the attribution, zone and datum in the notes. Times are the Marine Institute's own 5-minute values, never interpolated.
- **Time zone:** ERDDAP times are UTC instants. The zone to show them in is `IRELAND_ZONE` = `Europe/Dublin` (05:15Z is 05:15 in November, 06:15 in July: tested), explicit in the result so the card cannot fall back to the viewer's zone.
- **Datum, unresolved:** the variable `Water_Level_ODMalin` carries `sea_level_datum = "OD Malin"` and `units` metres, but the dataset `summary` says "Water Level is sea surface height above local Lowest Astronomical Tide". They contradict. The reader labels the datum from the variable (OD Malin) and its note says the summary differs and is unconfirmed: **times alone are safe; do not show heights as chart-datum depths until the Marine Institute confirms.** (Predicted Lahinch heights run from about -1.85 to +1.91 m, which is a mean-sea-level-like datum, not LAT, where every value would be positive.)
- Recorded fixtures: `imi-stations-recorded.csv` (the list) and `imi-hilo-lahinch-recorded.csv` (2026-11-10 05:15Z High 1.91 m, 11:25Z Low -1.85, 17:35Z High 1.67, 23:35Z Low -1.696, then 11-11). Live result: Lahinch's next water is the 11:25Z low.
