# World spot catalogues: quality audit, 2026-11-09

Independent audit of the 13 merged world catalogues in this folder (branch `world-audit`, after merging `forecast-trust`). Every check below was run against the files and live public pages on 2026-11-09; scripts and raw outputs were kept in the session scratchpad (`audit/`).

## Summary
- **Rows audited:** 2536 kept spots across 13 files. After fixes: 2532 (4 duplicates removed and moved to `dropped`).
- **Random sample (60 spots, re-fetched):** 3 of 60 failed one check; all 3 failures are the same France degree-rounding defect (fixed). Facing word, position, time zone and two Surfnerd-attributed facts matched the live page for the other 57. A second-guide check (one Surf-Forecast.com-attributed fact, verbatim in the live page) passed 43 of 43 spots that have a Surf-Forecast source.
- **Objective fixes applied:** 371 field or row changes (list below): France 16-point degrees, empty attributed values, 4 duplicate rows. No published fact was re-estimated.
- **Open (not fixable without a new source):** 181 rows have `type: null` (no published break type), 71 pairs of differently named spots share or nearly share a pin, and 41 pins could not be confirmed against the OSM coastline because every Overpass server timed out.

## 1. Random sample: re-fetch and verify
**Method.** 60 kept spots drawn at random (Python `random.Random(20261109)`), allocated to files in proportion to their size (largest remainder). For each spot the Surfnerd page in its `facingSource` was re-fetched as Markdown (`<url>.md`, 1 request per 1.1 s) and compared with the row:
- **Facing:** the page's "Faces X" equals the row's facing word, and `seaward` equals X on the 16-point rose (N = 0, NNE = 22.5 ...).
- **Position:** the row pin is within 1 km of the source named in `positionSource` (the Surfnerd front-matter latitude/longitude, or the OSM element's centroid via Nominatim lookup).
- **Time zone:** the row zone equals the Surfnerd page's `timezone` (or has identical UTC offsets every 3 days through 2026).
- **Two attributed facts:** the Surfnerd-attributed parts of `swellExposure` (direction sequence must equal the page's "Swell exposure" without "Faces X") and `windOffshore` (every direction must appear in the page's "Workable wind" or "Wind exposure").
- **Second guide (extra):** for spots with a Surf-Forecast.com source, one fact attributed to it (the quoted sentence, else the direction) must appear in the live page text.
