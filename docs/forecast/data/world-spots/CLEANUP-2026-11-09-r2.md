# World catalogue cleanup, round 2, 2026-11-09

Scope: the world-spot files that round 1 (`CLEANUP-2026-11-09.md`) did not audit. Data only; no `src/` change. No row was dropped or moved, and no `dropped` array was edited. Spot totals are unchanged.

## Method
Round 1's method, reused.
- **Break type (355 rows with `type: null`):** in Canada, Caribbean, China, India, Japan, Pacific Islands, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, US East, US Gulf and Vietnam.
  - Sources: surf-forecast.com break pages, found through its country and region break lists (US: East and Gulf regions only), and Wannasurf spot pages, found through its zone indexes. Both fetched with curl at about 1 request a second per site.
  - A guide page counts only if its own pin is within 6 km of the row's pin and its name matches.
  - `type` is set only when every matched guide states the same single Beach, Point or Reef, and Surfnerd states no type or the same one. A set type also gets `typeSource`.
  - Otherwise the guide wording goes into `facts.breakTypeText` and `type` stays null. Rows where only Surfnerd states a combined or other type (e.g. "Beach, Jetty") also get that wording, attributed to Surfnerd.
- **Pins more than 3 km from the coast:** 79 pins, measured against the Natural Earth 10 m coastline plus minor islands. They are in Pacific Islands, New Zealand, Maldives, Caribbean, Philippines, Ecuador, Netherlands, US Gulf, Brazil, Japan, Peru, US East and Vietnam.
  - Overpass timed out again on three mirrors, so two other OSM sources were used:
    - Round 1's Nominatim island, islet, beach and name search, bounded about 15 km around the pin, with polygon outlines.
    - The OSM API `map` call (bbox about ±5 km, `natural=coastline` ways with geometry). This is a direct check against the OSM coastline itself.
  - All 79 pins are within 1.5 km of an OSM `natural=coastline` way. So none was re-sourced and none dropped.
  - Each row gets `coastCheck`, and a note is appended to `positionSource.text`. No pin was moved.
