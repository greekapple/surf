# World spots: recovery with a computed coastline facing (2026-11-09)

Spec rule (docs/specs/2026-11-08-spot-expansion.md): "The facing (seaward degrees): from the guides, otherwise from the coastline." This pass applies the second half to rows that were dropped only for having no published facing. A recovered row carries `facingKind: "computed-coastline"`; its facing is a measurement of the OpenStreetMap coastline, never a guide fact, and `facts.facingDeg` stays null.

## Method

- **Input:** the `dropped` arrays of every region file (not `us-east-batch1-ids.json`). Candidates = rows with a `url` or `guidePin` whose drop reason is a missing facing (2,189). Rows dropped for other reasons (682 "facing is published but no break type", 37 duplicates, 1 time zone) were left alone. 2,370 guide pages (Surf-Forecast.com 1,192, Wannasurf 865, Surfnerd 324) were fetched with `curl -A Mozilla/5.0` at about 1 request a second per site; all returned 200.
- **Position:** the row `guidePin` when present; otherwise the pin published on the page (Surfnerd JSON-LD geo, Wannasurf GPS block, Surf-Forecast.com map location), preferring Surfnerd, then Wannasurf, then Surf-Forecast.com. `positionSource` names the site, the raw pin, its precision when stated and the distance to any other guide pin. Every candidate had a pin; the largest spread between two guide pins for one row was 2.4 km (a 5 km disagreement rule was in place and never fired). Stored rounded to 3 dp.
- **Coastline:** OSM `natural=coastline` as assembled into the ocean polygons of the OpenFreeMap planet vector tiles (OpenMapTiles `water` layer, `class=ocean`, zoom 12, planet snapshot 2026-11-01; these polygons are the osmdata.openstreetmap.de water polygons built from the coastline ways). Overpass could not be used: overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems and overpass.private.coffee timed out on TCP connect and maps.mail.ru returned 504 (2026-11-09); osmdata.openstreetmap.de also timed out. Sea lies right of every ring, the same convention as coastline ways (land on the left); a side check confirms a point 25 m seaward is inside the ocean polygon.
- **Facing:** nearest coastline point P to the pin; the coastline within 500 m of P is cut into pieces of at most 10 m; the length-weighted mean direction of travel, rotated 90 deg to the right (toward the sea), is `seaward`, kept to 0.1 deg (not rounded to a 16-point rose).
- **Reject rules (row stays dropped, reason in `recoveryAttempt`):** nearest coastline more than 1.5 km from the pin (this includes every Great Lakes shore: lake water is not `natural=coastline`); segments within 500 m disagree by more than 45 deg, measured as the length-weighted mean deviation from the mean direction (arccos of the mean resultant length; headland, cove or point); enclosed water: 13 rays from 30 m offshore across seaward +-90 deg all hit land within 2 km (lagoon, estuary or harbour less than 2 km across); a same-name spot already in the file (or recovered earlier in this pass) within 10 km (possible duplicate listing).
- **Cross-check (`facingCheck`):** every published direction on the pages: Surfnerd offshore wind and best swell, Surf-Forecast.com offshore wind and best swell, Wannasurf good wind and good swell. A source passes when any listed wind direction is within 60 deg of facing+180 and any listed swell direction within 90 deg of the facing. Any failing source keeps the row dropped with "computed facing contradicts published offshore wind/swell"; the facing is never adjusted. Rows whose pages give no direction are recovered with `facingCheck.result` saying so.
- **Facts:** published facts from the pages in the guide-facts schema, each attributed (ideal swell, wind, tide, bottom, break type text, wave direction, size, exposure, hazards, season, skill, frequency). `type` is set only when a page states Beach, Point or Reef (Surfnerd type, Surf-Forecast.com type, Wannasurf Beach-break / Reef-* / Point-break), otherwise null.
- **Time zone:** `timezonefinder` (timezone-boundary-builder polygons) at the pin; checked against the Surfnerd page zone where present (no disagreement). Country from the kept rows of the same file for that zone, else the guide page. Region from the guide page (`regionSource`).
- **nearestSources:** nearest NDBC station from activestations.xml (within 600 km; live = wave height in NDBC latest_obs), nearest IOC sea-level station (within 400 km; live = last sample within 24 h of 2026-11-10 04:40 UTC), nearest IEM ASOS/METAR airport (within 200 km, IEM online flag); great-circle km. `nearestCatalogued` gives the closest spot already in the file.
- **Calibration before the run:** the same computation on 300 random kept spots with a published facing. Under the final rules 202 were computable; 202 of 202 (100%) fall within 30 deg of the published facing (median difference 6.6 deg). The published facings are 16-point words, so up to 11.25 deg of each difference is their rounding.

## Limits

- Guide pins are not surveyed takeoffs; Surf-Forecast.com notes some of its pins are approximate and all Wannasurf pins but a few say "Approximate". The facing describes the coastline nearest the pin.
- A 500 m mean direction describes the beach; it can differ from where a reef or point break actually takes swell. Points and headlands mostly fail the 45 deg rule and stay dropped.
- Wannasurf "good wind" lists are not strictly offshore; they were used as published, which can only make the check stricter.
- Scripts and caches lived in the agent scratchpad (not committed): page parsing, tile decoding, the computation and the QA sample.
