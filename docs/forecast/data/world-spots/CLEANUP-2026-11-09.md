# World catalogue cleanup, 2026-11-09 (spec 2C)

Scope: the 13 files audited in `AUDIT-2026-11-09.md`. Data only; no `src/` change. Spot totals unchanged: no row was dropped or moved.

## Method
- **Break type (183 rows with `type: null`).**
  - Sources: surf-forecast.com break pages, found through its per-region break lists, and Wannasurf spot pages, found through its zone indexes. Both fetched with curl at about 1 request a second per site.
  - A guide page counts only if its own pin is within 6 km of the row's pin and its name matches.
  - `type` is set only when a guide states a single Beach, Point or Reef ("beach break" / `Beach`; `Reef`, Reef-coral or Reef-rocky; `Point`, Point-break), and no matched guide (Surfnerd included) says otherwise.
  - Combined, river, breakwater and sand-bar wording is copied into `facts.breakTypeText`, and `type` stays null. A set type also gets `typeSource`.
- **Pins more than 3 km from the coast.** Measured against the Natural Earth 10 m coastline plus minor islands: 42 pins in these files.
  - Overpass (OSM `natural=coastline`) timed out on every mirror, so the OSM check used Nominatim instead.
  - Island, islet and beach searches, plus the spot name, were bounded about 15 km around the pin. Polygon outlines came from `/lookup?polygon_geojson=1`, and the pin's distance to the nearest outline was measured.
  - Every pin was within 1.5 km of an OSM land or beach outline, so none was re-sourced or dropped. Each row gets `coastCheck`, and a note is appended to `positionSource.text`. No pin was moved.
