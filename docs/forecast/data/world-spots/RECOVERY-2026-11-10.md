# World spots: recovery of dropped rows with no guide URL field (2026-11-10)

The 2026-11-09 recovery (`RECOVERY-2026-11-09.md`) only took dropped rows with a `url` or `guidePin` field. This pass takes the rest: **2,679 dropped rows with no `url`, `guidePin` or `recoveryAttempt`** in every region file except `us-west.json`, `alaska.json` (fresh) and `us-east-batch1-ids.json` (an id list). For each row it finds the guide page, then applies the approved 2026-11-09 steps unchanged: position from the guide pin, facing from a published Surfnerd "Faces" line first and otherwise computed from the OSM coastline with the same reject rules, a cross-check against the published wind and swell, attributed facts, `nearestSources` with km, and the IANA zone. Nothing is invented. Recovered rows move from `dropped` to `spots` with a unique id. Every row still dropped now has `recoveryAttempt` (`on` 2026-11-10, `method`, `result`) with its reason.

## Finding the guide page

- **Pages named in the drop reason.** 2,676 of the 2,679 rows already name their guide page(s) in the `reason` text or a `urls` list, though not in a `url` field. These were the pages the region agents matched; they are used as found.
- **Pages found by name.** Exact slug matches of the row name, the name without a parenthesis, and the parenthesised alias, against the full Surfnerd spot sitemap (`sitemap-spots.xml`, 7,455 spots) and the Surf-Forecast.com sitemaps (`sitemaps/A-A.xml.gz` … `Z-Z.xml.gz`, 8,033 `/breaks/` slugs). Compound names are not split on "-", "/" or "or": a part of a compound name is often a different break nearby. This gave 459 candidate pages for 350 rows that the drop reason does not name.
- **Acceptance.** A name-matched page counts only when its pin lies within 5 km of a position for the row: the row `pin`, the pin quoted in the drop reason, or the pin of a page the reason names. That position is used only when it lies in a country of the file or of the page's own country label, so `Kamala` (Thailand), whose quoted pin was in Mali, was not used as an anchor. 387 name matches failed the 5 km test. Twelve rows had no position at all, so their name matches were **not** accepted: a country check alone let "Kammies (Maui)" match Oahu's Kammies in a first run. If a site gave two different candidate pages for one row, the row was skipped as ambiguous (6). If a page was already the source of a kept spot, the row stays dropped as a duplicate listing (48).
- **Wannasurf** has no name-addressable URL and its robots.txt excludes the directory, so it was not searched by name. Wannasurf pages named in drop reasons (340) were used.
- **Fetching.** 3,047 pages were fetched with `curl -A Mozilla/5.0`, at most one request start every 1.1 s per host, and all returned 200. 47 pages were reused from the 2026-11-09 cache. Open-Meteo was not called.

## Steps after the page (unchanged from 2026-11-09)

- **Position:** the page pin (Surfnerd JSON-LD, then Wannasurf GPS, then Surf-Forecast.com), rounded to 3 dp. If two guides' pins are more than 5 km apart, the row stays dropped. `positionSource` gives the distance to the position quoted in the drop reason when there was one.
- **Facing.** A published Surfnerd "Faces X" counts only when it comes with a swell-exposure range (for example "ESE - SSW, (Faces SSE)"), as on the kept spots. A bare "(Faces X)" with "Workable swell: Unknown" was already judged "no facing" by the region agents (La Flotte, Puerto Caldera, El Anclote), and that decision stands. Otherwise the facing is computed from the coastline: the OpenFreeMap z12 ocean polygons (OSM `natural=coastline`, planet snapshot 2026-11-01), using the same tile cache and code as 2026-11-09. A row is rejected when the nearest coastline is more than 1.5 km away, when the segments within 500 m deviate by more than 45 deg, or when the point is on enclosed water. It is also rejected when a same-name spot lies within 10 km, or when any spot lies within 50 m (a possible duplicate). A computed row carries `facingKind: "computed-coastline"` and `facts.facingDeg: null`. A row with a published facing has no `facingKind`, and its `facts.facingDeg` is the 16-point degree value.
- **Cross-check:** every published offshore/good wind must be within 60 deg of facing+180, and every best/good swell within 90 deg of the facing, for every source. Any failure keeps the row dropped. This also applies to published facings (3 kept dropped).
- **Facts, zone, sources:** the same builder as 2026-11-09. Facts are attributed to their site. `timezonefinder` gives the zone at the pin. `nearestSources` lists the nearest NDBC station (600 km), IOC gauge (400 km) and IEM METAR airport (200 km), with great-circle km.
- **New field `guideMatch`** on each recovered row: `namedInDropReason` (the URLs taken from the reason) and `foundByName` (URL plus the pin-distance note).

## Quality gate: 20 random recovered rows

- **Against the guides:** none of the 20 pages states a facing in words (why they were dropped); all 20 publish an offshore wind and/or best swell direction, and all 20 are consistent with the computed facing under the cross-check rule (by construction, failing rows were not recovered).
- **Guide-page match:** the page title names the row (full name, base name or alias) on 20/20; 9 rows had a position in the drop reason and the page pin is 0.18 to 1.35 km from it; 11 had none.

## Limits

- Guide pins are not surveyed takeoffs. The facing describes the coastline nearest the pin, and a 500 m mean direction describes the beach, not necessarily a reef or point takeoff.
- Most recoveries (1,086) rest on pages the region agents had already matched by name. This pass did not re-judge those matches. It re-checked only that the page pin lies in a country of the file and agrees with the other guides' pins within 5 km.
- Name matching is exact-slug only. It finds a page only when the guide spells the name the same way, so spelling variants were not searched.
- Scripts and caches are in the agent scratchpad (`rec2/`) and are not committed: targets, fetch, stage2, assembly, verification and QA.
