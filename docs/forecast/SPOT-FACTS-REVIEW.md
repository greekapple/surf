**What this is.** Every value below was read from a published source (or, for one facing, Pismo’s, derived from CDIP agency data) (user decision, 2026-11-02: look at public sources and apply them) and carries its URL and retrieval date. The pages were read through page summaries by automated fetch tools, NOT verified against the full pages, and none of it is surveyed or reviewed by a person. Spot-guide facts (facing, swell range, tide, bottom, size, hazards) come from Surfnerd and Surf-Forecast.com spot guides (T-073 adds Wannasurf and The Surf Atlas) and are labelled provenance “published guide”; where no guide states a single facing, the facing may be derived from coastline geometry (CDIP MOP shore normals) and is labelled “derived from coastline geometry”; the minimum swell period is a generic published threshold, not specific to the spot. A review is optional: tick “change to” on any row to replace a value. Where the guides disagree, the narrower swell window and the more cautious exposure word are used and the disagreement is in the note. A spot gets a surf estimate only if it has a facing and a swell range (points, reefs and jetties also need a source that says exposed); a spot a source calls sheltered estimates only through its CDIP nearshore point (never the offshore-swell v1) and only inside the swell directions a guide lists it as open to, and a spot whose published working swell wraps onto it from outside ±60° of its facing refuses (T-073, docs/forecast/ESTIMATE-COVERAGE-T073.md); the rating rules are unchanged. One side effect to know about: a published facing lets the rating tell offshore from onshore wind at the spots that now have one (39 instead of 1 at T-071, 44 at T-073), so ratings there shifted: across all spots in daylight hours, SOLID or better went from 9.1% to 20.7% of spot-hours (docs/forecast/ESTIMATE-COVERAGE-T071.md).

Where no source states a fact, the row says “none found” and the fact stays unknown.
