A review is optional: tick “change to” on any row to replace a value. Where the guides disagree, the narrower swell window and the more cautious exposure word are used and the disagreement is in the note. A spot gets a surf estimate only if a source states its facing and swell range and none calls it sheltered (points, reefs and jetties also need a source that says exposed); the rating rules are unchanged.

Where no source states a fact, the row says “none found” and the fact stays unknown.
