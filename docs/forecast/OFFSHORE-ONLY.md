**What this is.** The headline is a MOP value where a CDIP MOP point is assigned (California), else our v1 breaking estimate where the spot's facts allow it, else the labelled offshore height. A spot is offshore-only (a) STATICALLY when a fact v1 needs is missing (beach orientation, breaking profile, shelter) or (b) HOURLY when the facts exist but fewer than half of its daylight hours in the capture get an estimate (swell outside the window, under the minimum period, wind sea). The hourly layer covers only the spots in the capture (the shown captures hold 48 forecast hours, not 7 days, and 492 of the 1078 spots); spots with facts but no capture are listed as profile-ok-no-capture, not classified.

## Step 3: the NWS surf forecast as the `official` headline (offshore-surf)
Where we have no surf estimate for an hour (the offshore fallback, and a MOP spot whose nearshore data is missing), and the spot's NWS zone is mapped (`src/data/srfZones.ts`, generated by `scripts/build-srf-zones.mjs` from `surf-height-sources.json`), the headline is the NWS Surface Zone Forecast range for the spot's local day: **kind `official`**, in `src/services/officialSurf.ts`.
- **Served**: the hourly capture (services/surf-capture.mjs) also writes the newest parsed product per office to R2 `serve/srf/<OFFICE>.json`; the Worker serves it at `/api/srf?office=MTR` (services/srf-serve.mjs; an R2 failure is a 503, never "no product"). The page loads each scoped spot's office product into `bundle.srf` (src/ocean/pipeline.ts).
- **Rules**: the product's own period date must match the spot's local date (nothing past its periods); the facing entry is the one nearest the spot's seaward bearing within 45° (a tie, mismatch or unlabelled mix refuses); "N feet or less" shows "up to N ft"; unreadable text shows nothing; a product older than its zone's next scheduled issue (the UGC expiry) plus 6 h is shown as stale.
- **Labelled** "NWS surf forecast: 4–7 ft (zone), issued Wed 4:35 AM PST"; tags "NWS" on day cards, week cells, map labels, Compare; share text "NWS surf fcst 4–7 ft". Never converted to a face or Hawaiian scale (metres are a plain conversion), never part of our rating, never recorded in the forecast archive that scores our forecasts, never drawn as hourly values on the chart.
- **Not in this change**: SWAN stays off; the Atlantic 8 s minimum-period question (97 spots) is a rule change that needs the user's approval, with the srf-compare numbers as evidence once this is live.
- **Served product only moves forward** (Codex P1): `serve/srf/<OFFICE>.json` is written only when the product's issuance is newer than the one the index says is served; a backlog drained afterwards (older products) never overwrites it. Any backfill must use the same rule.
- **Honolulu half-days** (Codex P2): the AM/PM halves of the HFO table are kept distinct. An hour takes its own half; a day with different halves reads "NWS AM 4–6 ft · PM 6–8 ft" (week cell "4–6 / 6–8"); a half is never presented as the whole day, and the "Tonight" (night) columns are never used for the day.
