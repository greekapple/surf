# Catalogue pin check (pin-check, 2026-11-13)

The coast-tile build (`tools/coast-tiles/build-coast-tiles.py`, OSM land polygons, ODbL) measures each California pin against the mapped shore. **43 pins are on the sea side more than 200 m from the shore (13 of them 300 to 800 m), and 3 are more than 200 m inside the land polygon** (54 pins are inside it in all; the other 51 by 200 m or less, the simplification and the polygons' own error). This sheet checks the 46 against the guides' own coordinates.

**Method.** The distance is from the pin to the nearest edge of the simplified tile polygon (25 m simplification, so about ±25 m). Each spot's own guide pages (the URLs in `docs/forecast/data/guide-facts.json`: surfnerd, and surf-forecast where it has the spot) were fetched with curl, and the pin in the page (`lat`/`lng`) was read and placed on the tile. **A pin moves only when the guides agree with each other and put it clearly nearer the real shore, or when ours is on land and the guides agree where it should be.** One pin moved. The catalogue's pins came from surfnerd, so surfnerd's pin is within about 60 m of ours for nearly every spot (it cannot contradict us); surf-forecast's pins are often a km or more off (see the note in the spot-guide notes), so one surf-forecast pin against ours does not move anything.

## What this does and does not say

- A guide pin is where the guide's author clicked, usually the take-off or the middle of the break, not a survey. A pin 200 to 400 m out for a sandbar beach break or a rivermouth is normal (the break is on the outer bar); a reef or point sits where the reef is. Pins 400 m or more off the shore for a beach (Shark Pit Marin 800 m, Tunitas Creek 566, McGrath 489, Centerville 456) are flagged "large offset": the guides (where they agree) put them there too, so they were left.
- `cs-moonstone` and `cs-shelter-cove` are on land by more than 200 m and were not moved because there is no agreeing second source; both are worth one look at the chart.
- Nothing else changed: no MOP assignment, NWS zone, rating or number moved. The coast tiles of the 45 unmoved spots are untouched.
