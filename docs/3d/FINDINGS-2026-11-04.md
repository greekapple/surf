# 3D wave scene: findings before the "real surfing" work (2026-11-04, Fable)

Brief: `docs/specs/2026-11-04-3d-real-surfing.md`, priority 1 (surfer movement). Everything below was measured on the round-3 build
(forecast-trust `db23d9f`) in headed Chrome on the real GPU with the scripts in the session scratchpad (`measure-r3*.mjs`): per-frame
world and local joint rotations, board rotation, board-to-water gaps at the nose, tail and both rails, the rider against the break point
and the lip, at the forecast hour (Ocean Beach, 3.05 m face) and at forced 2, 6, 12 and 20 ft faces.

## 1. How the scene is built today
- **Water.** One grid strip (`NX`=384 along the crest x `NU`=160 across) whose vertex shader (`STRIP_VS` in `WaveLive.tsx`) samples a
  Blender-built break-cycle atlas (`waveAtlas.ts`, 12 stages x 96 arc-length points per size bucket, float16, licensed-derived and
  git-ignored) by (u along the profile, `stageAt(x, t)`), times an along-crest height envelope `ampAt(x)`. The crest frame moves with the
  peel: x = 0 is the break point, the shoulder is at -x, the water texture streams past at `uFlowX`. The two buckets around the hour's
  face height are blended, the period rescales the cycle, the breaker type picks the water shader's foam, the wind the chop, the sun the
  light. The CPU mirror of the same maths (`stageAt`, `ampAt`, `profileInto`, `facePoint`, `waterHeightAt`) places the rider and the spray.
- **Rider.** `surfer-v3.glb` (CC0 MPFB2 human, 24 bones, a non-deforming `board` bone with the board mesh under it) with two clips: `ride`
  (4.04 s, 97 keys at 24 fps, six IK-posed keys baked to FK: trim, bottom, climb, top, recover, pump, trim) and `still`. An
  `AnimationMixer` plays `ride` in a loop at a time scale that fits a whole number of loops into the wave cycle.
- **Ride line (round 3).** `RIDE_WP`: nine (phase, x offset in face heights, height fraction) waypoints, a closed Catmull-Rom loop,
  sampled into a 128-entry table per hour (`buildRide`), clamped under a 78 deg slope cap and at least one face height ahead of the earliest
  break point, then smoothed. The same loop every hour; only its period follows the wave cycle.
- **Placement (`placeRider`, every render frame).** Position = `facePoint(x, frac, t)` (one point on the face). Heading F = the along-crest
  velocity of the table plus the face point's motion over 1/15 s. Deck normal = the face normal in the profile plane made perpendicular to
  F, then rolled about F by a lean from the line's lateral acceleration (`atan(0.65 a/g)`, capped 18 deg). The board's bottom centre is then
  shifted vertically by its gap to `waterHeightAt`, and lifted further when any of four probe points is more than 14 cm under.
- **Body layers (every frame, after the mixer).** The torso bone (`spine_01`) is counter-rotated by the deck tilt beyond 42 deg
  (`torsoS` spring), the spine/neck/head yaw toward the heading 0.2 s ahead (`leadS`), both upper arms rotate about F by the lean. Each is
  applied with `rotBone`, which **premultiplies** the bone's current quaternion.
- **Camera, spray, labels, lighting** are unchanged by this work and are not part of the problem.
- **Inputs.** Face height = `WaveSpec.faceM` (the estimate's centre, "drawn at X ft face"); the person's height `surferM`; `UserProfile.skill`
  (Beginner / Intermediate / Expert; the default profile is Intermediate) exists but is **not used by the scene yet**; stance does not exist
  in the profile; period, breaker type, wind and direction already drive the water. There is no forecast-hour seed; the clip phase is wall time.

## 2. The actual cause of each glitch (measured)

### Pose popping (arms jump, torso snaps)
**Cause: the procedural layers accumulate on the clip for the first 1/24 s of every loop.** The glTF `ride` sampler's first key is at
t = 0.0417 s (Blender frame 1), not 0. While the action time is below the first key, three.js clamps the clip to that key, so the evaluated
quaternion is bit-identical frame to frame, and `PropertyMixer.apply` **skips writing a bone whose value did not change**. The viewer then
premultiplies its torso/head/arm rotations onto the *previous frame's already-rotated* quaternion, so they add up once per frame, and snap
back the moment the keys resume.
- Verified: with the torso layer switched off, the `spine_01` local step at the wrap is 0.0 deg; with it on, 12.5 deg then 9.9 deg in two
  consecutive frames at 60 Hz, and the first step equals the layer's own angle `over` (12.5 deg) exactly. All other inputs (F, U, lean,
  lead, pelvis and board world rotation) move 1.2 deg or less in those frames. The clip itself closes (first vs last key 0.0 deg on every
  track, biggest key-to-key step 4 deg on `spine_01`, 20 deg on `upperarm_l`).
- On a 200 Hz display (the sibling worktree's build, where the user watched) eight frames accumulate: every joint jumped 70-172 deg in
  one frame at phase 0.011 at the forecast hour and at 6, 12 and 20 ft (13 joints over 25 deg per loop); 0 at 2 ft, where the deck tilt is
  under 42 deg and the torso layer is idle. The arms carry the torso jump (they are its children) plus their own lean term.
- Secondary: the torso counter-rotation is applied to **one bone** and reaches 42 deg (measured `over` 0.735 rad at the top turn), so the
  upper body folds at a single joint, which also reads as "leaning back off the tail / lying on the board".

### Physically wrong poses
- The clip's six keys are reasonable on their own (they were posed against photos), but the viewer adds a 0-42 deg single-joint torso
  fold, a yaw lead of up to 28 deg split across spine_03/neck/head, and arm rotations from the **line's lateral acceleration**, which is the
  second difference of a smoothed 128-sample table: the measured lean swings -17 -> +15 -> -8 deg within 0.6 s at the 6 ft top turn and
  -18 -> +15 deg at 20 ft, so the arms and the rail "flail" with it. Nothing balances the centre of mass over the board: the head sits
  0.8-1.5 m toward the beach of the board on a 55-70 deg face (measured head vs board cross-shore positions at every height).

### The board does not sit on the water
**Cause: single-point placement with a profile-plane normal, then a vertical lift.** The board is placed from one face point and one 2D
normal, F is pitched by the line's climb, and the surface under the nose and tail is never sampled. The four-point rail cap (any point
14 cm under lifts the whole board) then fires almost every frame (`low_m` sits at -0.14 m in nearly every sample at every height).
- Measured deck pitch relative to the water along the board (nose gap minus tail gap over the length): +8 deg at 6 ft trim, +16 deg at the
  forecast hour, +24 deg at 20 ft; the nose is 0.26-0.59 m out of the water while the tail is 14 cm under. Centre gap ("floating") up to
  0.18 m at the forecast hour, 0.34 m at 6 ft, 0.72 m at 20 ft (nose 1.3-1.5 m out at the 20 ft top turn).
- Board yaw: heading error against the measured velocity peaks at 47 deg (6 ft, climbing) and 21 deg (20 ft): F mixes the table's
  along-crest velocity with the wave's own pulse motion (`sections`) sampled 1/15 s apart.

### The surfer drifts onto the back or top of the wave
- Not reproduced as "behind the crest" in this build: in 5 s at each of the four heights the head stayed 0.9-2.5 m toward the beach of
  the lip. What is measured: at 6 ft the head pokes up to 0.21 m **above the lip** for ~15% of the loop (the wave is 1.83 m, the rider
  1.75 m, at 30% up the face), and at the top turn the rider holds frac 0.64-0.65 of trough-to-lip on a 70-71 deg wall with the board
  rolled 18 deg and the torso folded 42 deg. The round-3 ride line also carries the rider 0.2-0.5 m toward the beach of the lip line at the
  top turn (`boardCross` 0.0-0.3 m vs `lipCross` -0.2 m), so with the counter-rotated torso the body is over the flats rather than on the face.

### No sense of a line
**Cause: the ride loop is fixed in the crest frame and ignores the curl.** x = `surferX` + the loop's offset, clamped once per hour to one
face height ahead of the *earliest* break point; the break point `breakX(t)` then wanders with the section pulses (-0.2..+0.7 m at 6 ft,
-0.2..+1.7 m at 20 ft) while the rider does not move with it. The rider is 5.1-7.0 m ahead of the break point at every height, i.e.
2.8 face heights at 6 ft but only 1.1 at 20 ft, so the pocket is never ridden and the relation to the breaking section changes with size
by accident. There is no takeoff, no maneuver structure beyond the six keys, and speed is whatever the loop's period gives.

### Also found
- Per-frame allocations in `placeRider` / `contactM` / `probe` (arrays and objects each frame); `Math.random` in the spray only.
- The water time is wall-clock, so a frame hitch moves the wave more than the rider.
- No determinism: the clip phase and the loop are wall-time.

## 3. Plan per stage (this run covers 1-4; the band A/B/C profiles are the next run)
1. **This note, then tooling.** `surfSceneConfig.ts` holds every tunable (thresholds, spring rates, stance widths, board dimensions, skill
   profiles, maneuver shapes, monitor limits, the A/B/C band table shape). Dev-only URL params and a panel force face (ft), skill, stance,
   direction and seed (the label says "dev: forced" when a value is forced). Glitch monitor: joint and board angular change per frame
   (threshold 25 deg per 1/60 s, scaled by the frame's dt), board centre gap and lowest-point gap, `console.warn` with maneuver, time and joint,
   counts on `window.__wave.glitches()`. A 6 ft scale pole (1 ft bands) toggled by `?pole=1` / the panel / `__wave.setPole`.
2. **Board on the water.** A fixed 120 Hz simulation with interpolated render; the wave time comes from the simulation clock. The board is
   placed by `waterHeightAt` (the CPU mirror of the vertex shader's `P()`) at the centre, nose, tail and both rails: pitch and roll are the
   fitted plane, yaw follows the planned velocity, rail roll = atan(v^2/(g r)) from the planned speed and turn rate (capped), the
   centre stays on the surface and a rolled rail may bury up to the configured depth. Orientation through critically damped springs on the
   fixed step; no per-frame randomness. The mixer is dropped: the clip's six keys become a pose library sampled once at load, so nothing is
   premultiplied onto a stale mixer write (the root cause above cannot recur).
3. **Procedural body.** Pose blending with 150-400 ms cross-fades per maneuver; two-bone leg IK pinning both feet to the deck at the stance
   positions; compression/extension scaling the hip height (deep into the bottom turn, extended out of it, compressed before the top turn);
   centre of mass over the board (the body rides the rolled deck, the torso counter-tilt spread over three spine bones and limited);
   head and shoulders lead the planned heading, hips follow; arms counter-balance the roll; joint limits on every procedural addition;
   low-frequency seeded idle sway. Stance regular/goofy with frontside/backside from stance x direction (backside = the rider mirrored
   across the board's long axis). Stance is **not in the profile**: a seeded default per spot-hour, recorded as an assumption.
4. **Line planner.** Wave-relative (u, v): u = distance from the moving curl `breakX(t)` in face heights (followed through a spring so the
   rider does not twitch with every pulse), v = height on the face, clamped under the rideable slope cap per step. The ride is a loop of
   maneuver segments (bottom turn, top turn, recovery, trim, cutback, pump; each a cubic Hermite in u and v with a duration), chosen and
   jittered by the forecast-hour seed; the skill sets the pocket distance (Expert tight, Intermediate mid, Beginner out on the shoulder)
   and the line height. The rider is never behind the break point. The band A/B/C style profiles are defined as data in the config (shape
   only, next run fills them).
5. Verification as in the brief: 5 s frame-by-frame per skill at 2/6/12/20 ft, Left and Right, zero monitor warnings; the pole against the
   label; never overtaken by the whitewater; a contact sheet and 4 s clips at 1440 and 390; fps on the real GPU (worst frame).

## 5. Run 3 (branch `3d-real-surfing-3`, 2026-11-04 night): the open heading item, takeoff/kick-out, bands, wake, dev UI
- **Heading (solved).** Not the face sampling: the water flow was taken along the bare crest axis, but near the curl the face at a
  given height bends 15-25 deg toward the beach, so a quarter of the "velocity over the water" pointed through the face and the board
  (which lies in the face) could never follow it. The water now streams along the face's own x-tangent (dP/dx at fixed v, the line the
  shader offsets its texture along, `rideSim.step`); the board axis is the water-relative velocity in the face plane. Measured in headed
  Chrome: 12 ft Expert 44% -> 0% of visible samples over 15 deg, 20 ft Expert 26% -> 0%, 20 ft Intermediate 22% -> 0%. The residual
  (max 10-20 deg) is the top-turn apex, where the water-relative speed drops to 2-4 m/s; the cap's soft limit was widened
  (`RIDE.capSoftV`) so the apex is a turn, not a 6 g reversal.
- **Rail depth (solved).** `sagOf` had the concavity sign backwards: on every hollow (concave) face the rail allowance was 0, so the
  Expert rails sat 12-16 cm in. Now: the board's plane is the one through its four contact points; the centre lifts off a concave
  section by the measured rail/end excess (the "bridge", up to 10 cm, an allowance the monitor subtracts); the roll cap works from
  the rail depth measured last step at the monitor's own probe points (`BOARD.probe`, shared with `contactM`), with a 2 cm margin,
  and the roll spring is clamped to the cap. Every cell <= 8.8 cm in the browser.
- **Takeoff and kick-out.** The plan is takeoff -> body -> exit -> (unseen) back to the takeoff; `S.VIS` fades the materials. Beginners
  straighten out; the others kick out over the lip. Period 8-25 s (longer on bigger faces).
- **Bands and expertise (brief stage 5)** as data: `MANEUVERS` (shapes relative to a band's line), `BANDS` (move sequences with weights,
  line height, pocket distance, compression, drop steepness, board range and types), `SKILLS.allow/pocketScale/intensity/slow`
  (filter and scale), `composeBody` (the composition). `boardForBand` picks the quiver board; the model's board is scaled otherwise.
  Intermediate gets the band C high line instead of a barrel (the brief's "shallow barrel" needs a drawn tube first: stage 8).
- **Wake and spray (brief stage 6).** `WAKE` in the config; the ribbon samples are (x, v) re-seated on the face each frame.
- **Dev UI.** The panel mounts only with `?dev=1`, the pole or a forced value; 44 px labels when shown.
- **Still off / next:** the Expert "barrel" is a high line under the lip, not inside a drawn tube (stage 8 wave shape); the heading
  check compares the board axis to the full 3D water-relative velocity, which includes the face's own morph (small, under 0.1 m/s);
  the straighten-out is a fade on the face, not a ride into the foam; one stray wide ribbon segment was seen once at 6 ft near the
  foam and not reproduced in a dump of the ribbon; the 20 ft Expert takeoff is a fast slide down the face rather than an air drop.
