# Audit: Map / Explore (live), 2026-11-10

Scope: `/explore` (layers, pins, coast pills, time bar, point label and panel, map search, focus chip/×/Esc, region chips), `/explore/compare`, `/explore/discover`.
Live site: https://swello.surf-prototype.workers.dev (headless Chrome via Playwright, Cloudflare Access service token).
Viewports: 1440×900, 390×844, 844×390; light and dark. Areas: California, Hawaii, East Coast (SE + NE), z3 to z11.
Evidence: screenshots in `/private/tmp/claude-501/audit-map/shots/`, scripts and logs in `/private/tmp/claude-501/audit-map/`.

**Rendering.** The first passes used SwiftShader; everything from the pin-rating check onward used the real GPU (ANGLE Metal, Apple M2). Items 3 and 9 were confirmed on the GPU.
**Machine load.** Other agents kept the load average at 14 to 29 throughout, so absolute timings (items 8 and 9) run pessimistic. The relative findings stand.

## Pins agree with the spot page (no P0 here)
I sampled 13 spots at Wed 11 Nov, 12 PM PST (10 AM HST / 3 PM EST): Ocean Beach, Linda Mar, Steamer Lane, Capitola, Mavericks, Rodeo Beach, Bolinas, Rincon, Sebastian Inlet, Hookipa, 16th Ave. Belmar, Halena Beach and Kepuhi Bay. The only mismatch was transient: Halena Beach read "No forecast record at this hour" until its data arrived (item 6).

### 3. P1: the sea field often fails to paint; it renders black and erases islands while the legend is showing
- **Where:** Explore, Waves layer. 1440 and 390, light and dark.
- **What:**
  - **Black sea:** on a fresh load at California z5, Hawaii z4/z6 and East Coast z6, the ocean is black with white particle streaks.
  - **Islands erased:** at Hawaii z6, Oahu, Maui, Kauai and Molokai disappear, leaving only the Big Island. At 390 z4 every island is gone.
  - **Bays painted black:** at California z7/z8 in SwiftShader runs, SF Bay is painted black too.
  - **Legend unaffected:** the legend (navy to crimson) stays up the whole time, so the screen suggests a reading that is not there.
  - **Not a SwiftShader artefact:** it reproduces on the Metal GPU (California z5 and Hawaii z6 both times; California z7 was flaky).
  - **Console:** every map load logs `GL_INVALID_OPERATION: glDrawArrays: Feedback loop formed between Framebuffer and active Texture`.
  - **Relation to known issues:** phone critique #4 listed this as unchecked ("timing artefact?"). It is not a timing artefact: the shots were taken 25–30 s after load with "Model data" showing.
- **Evidence:** `shots/gpu-gpu-hi6.png`, `shots/gpu-gpu-ca5.png`, `shots/zoom-1440-ca-z5.png`, `shots/zoom-1440-hi-z6.png`, `shots/zoom-390-hi-z4.png`, `shots/ui-1440-chip-SouthernCalifornia.png` (black bay).
- **Fix:**
  1. Find the pass that samples a texture while it is bound as the render target, probably the particle trail ping-pong (`src/map/field.ts` / SpatialLayer). Use separate read/write FBOs.
  2. Draw the particle layer above the field and below the land/labels, so it can never cover the basemap.
  3. Add a guard: if the field frame has not drawn, grey the legend and say "sea colour not drawn".
- **Effort:** M.
- **Re-verified:** yes, on 2 renderers and 2 runs each; California z7 is flaky.

### 4. P1: region chips never move the map (uncaught NaN error)
- **Where:** Explore, "N spots in view" list, region chips. 1440 (the code path is shared with the phone sheet).
- **What:**
  - **Map stays put:** clicking "Southern California", "Florida", "Hawaii · Maui" or "Hawaii · Oahu" filters the list but leaves the map and URL unchanged.
  - **Error:** each click throws `Error: Invalid LngLat object: (NaN, NaN)` at `cameraForBoxAndBearing`.
  - **Cause:** `fitPoints` passes `maxZoom: undefined` explicitly when the caller gives none (`src/map/engine/maplibreEngine.ts:59`). MapLibre then computes `Math.min(zoom, undefined)` = NaN. This confirms the known defect.
- **Evidence:** `shots/ui-1440-chip-SouthernCalifornia.png` (Southern California pressed, map still on the Bay Area); log `e-chip.mjs` (stack trace, url changed: false ×3).
- **Fix:** Build the options without the key (`...(opt.maxZoom !== undefined && {maxZoom: opt.maxZoom - ZOFF})`). Then check the other `fitPoints` callers.
- **Effort:** S.
- **Re-verified:** 2 runs, 4 chips.

### 8. P1: scrubbing the time bar janks badly
- **Where:** Explore time bar (keyboard scrub). 1440 and 390, Metal GPU.
- **What:**
  - **Wall time:** 24 ArrowRight steps sent 120 ms apart should take about 2.9 s; they took 8.4–9.7 s.
  - **Long tasks:** 44–59 long tasks, the longest 310–934 ms.
  - **Frames:** the worst frames were 350–1133 ms, with 31–39 frames over 50 ms (about 20 fps).
  - **Play:** 8 s of play advanced only 6 steps.

  RULES asks for a response within 100 ms. Machine load (avg ~14) inflates the absolute numbers, but long tasks of 300 ms+ are the app's own work.
- **Evidence:** `m-perf.mjs` output (4 runs); `ui-1440.log` "scrub 24 steps ms 6042 longtasks 33 max 406".
- **Fix:**
  1. Profile one scrub step.
  2. Batch the pin/pill DOM writes into one rAF.
  3. Compute ratings/labels per hour once and cache them.
  4. Skip label re-layout while dragging; settle on release.
- **Effort:** M.
- **Re-verified:** 4/4 runs.
